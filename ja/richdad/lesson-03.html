资产和负债，只有一条线Assets and Liabilities: One Line

你家的房子、爸爸的车、你的游戏机——哪些在往口袋里放钱，哪些在往外掏钱？Your family's home, the car, your games console — which ones put money in your pocket, and which take it out?

10分钟min1个原书章节source chapters3张记忆卡cards2道测验题quiz
故事Story

富爸爸说，整本书如果只留一条规则，就是这一条：分清资产和负债，然后买资产。他的定义简单到不像会计：资产往你口袋里放钱，负债从你口袋里拿钱。If only one rule survives from the whole book, rich dad said, it is this: know the difference between an asset and a liability, then buy assets. His definition is simpler than any accounting textbook: an asset puts money in your pocket, a liability takes money out.

为什么这么简单的事，大人却总搞错？因为大家看的是「东西值多少钱」，而不是「钱往哪个方向流」。一辆新车开出车行就掉价四分之一；一套自住的房子每个月要还房贷、交税、交保险、修屋顶。它们在账本上被写成资产，在现金流上却是负债。Why do grown-ups get this so wrong? Because they look at what a thing is worth instead of which way the money flows. A new car loses about a quarter of its price the moment it leaves the lot. A home you live in eats mortgage payments, taxes, insurance and roof repairs every month. On paper they are listed as assets; in cash flow they behave like liabilities.

书里画了三张现金流图。穷人：工资进来，全部变成支出流走。中产：工资进来，先变成房贷、车贷、信用卡这些负债，再流走。富人：资产栏先产生收入，收入再买更多资产，支出只是旁边小小的一条。The book draws three cash-flow pictures. Poor: income comes in and flows straight out as expenses. Middle class: income comes in, turns into mortgages, car loans and credit cards, then flows out. Rich: the asset column produces income first, that income buys more assets, and expenses are a small side stream.

所以书里那句最扎心的总结是：富人买资产；穷人只有支出；中产买他们以为是资产的负债。Hence the book's sharpest summary: the rich buy assets, the poor only have expenses, and the middle class buy liabilities they believe are assets.

书里还借了发明家巴克敏斯特·富勒的一个算法来定义财富：财富不是有多少钱，而是「如果你今天停止工作，能撑多少天」。一个月开销三千、存款三万的人，财富是三百天；一个月开销三千、每月租金收入四千的人，财富是无限天。The book also borrows a definition of wealth from the inventor Buckminster Fuller: wealth is not how much money you have, but how many days you could survive if you stopped working today. Spend 3,000 a month with 30,000 saved and your wealth is 300 days. Spend 3,000 a month with 4,000 of rent coming in and your wealth is unlimited days.

一句话The idea
别问它值多少钱，问它每个月往哪个方向搬钱。Don't ask what it is worth. Ask which way it moves money each month.
关键词Key words
资产Asset
往你口袋里放钱的东西：能收租的房子、会分红的股票、你写的书带来的版税、不用你守着的生意。Something that puts money in your pocket: a rented flat, shares that pay dividends, royalties from a book you wrote, a business that runs without you.
负债Liability
从你口袋里拿钱的东西：房贷、车贷、每月要续的会员、需要不断充值的游戏。Something that takes money out of your pocket: a mortgage, a car loan, a monthly subscription, a game that needs constant top-ups.
现金流Cash flow
钱流动的方向和路线。看一个人富不富，不看他的工资条，看他的钱从哪来、往哪去。The direction and route money travels. To see whether someone is wealthy, don't read their payslip — follow where their money comes from and where it goes.
财富 = 天数Wealth measured in days
如果今天不再工作，你的钱能撑多少天。这个算法把「降低支出」和「增加资产收入」变成了同一件事的两面。How many days your money lasts if you stop working today. This formula makes “spend less” and “earn more from assets” two sides of one move.

清单Lists
书里认可的真资产Assets the book actually counts
  1. 不需要你在场的生意（你必须天天上班的，那是一份工作）。Businesses that do not need you present — if you must work there daily, it is a job.
  2. 股票。Stocks.
  3. 债券。Bonds.
  4. 能产生租金收入的房产。Real estate that produces rent.
  5. 借条、欠条（别人欠你的钱，会付利息）。Notes and IOUs that pay you interest.
  6. 音乐、剧本、专利带来的版税。Royalties from music, scripts and patents.
  7. 任何有价值、能产生收入或升值、并且卖得掉的东西。Anything else with value that produces income or appreciates and has a ready market.
三张现金流图The three cash-flow pictures
  1. 穷人：收入 → 支出。钱只是路过。Poor: income → expenses. Money just passes through.
  2. 中产：收入 → 负债 → 支出。钱先变成账单。Middle class: income → liabilities → expenses. Money becomes bills first.
  3. 富人：资产 → 收入 → 更多资产。钱先变成会下蛋的鸡。Rich: assets → income → more assets. Money becomes a hen that lays eggs first.
生活里In real life

游戏皮肤和借出去的漫画Game skins and lent comics

每月要充值的皮肤是负债：它只会往外掏钱。你画的一本小漫画，同学排队花钱借来看，是资产：它在往你口袋里放钱。同样都是「喜欢的东西」，方向完全相反。A game skin that needs a monthly top-up is a liability: money only leaves. A little comic you drew that classmates pay to borrow is an asset: money arrives. Both are things you love. The direction is opposite.

自行车的两种命运Two lives of one bicycle

自己骑：要买锁、要修胎、要换零件，钱往外走。租给同学骑：每周收一点租金，钱往里走。同一辆车，用法不同，身份就不同。这就是为什么书里说「房子不一定是资产」。Ride it yourself and you buy locks, patch tyres and replace parts — money out. Rent it to classmates weekly and money comes in. Same bicycle, different use, different identity. That is why the book says a house is not automatically an asset.

「财富天数」小计算Your wealth in days

拿计算器算一下：你存了多少钱 ÷ 你每天大概花多少钱 = 你能撑多少天。再想想：如果每天有一块钱自动进账，这个数字会怎么变？Grab a calculator: money saved ÷ money spent per day = days you could last. Then ask: what happens to that number if one yuan arrives automatically every day?

问一问Questions
房子一定是负债吗？Is a house always a liability?
不一定。书里的判断标准只有一个：钱往哪流。自己住、每月还贷交税，就是负债；租出去、每月租金覆盖了所有开销还有剩，就是资产。同一栋房子，两种身份。No. The book uses one test only: which way does money flow? Live in it and pay a mortgage and taxes, and it is a liability. Rent it out so the rent covers every cost with something left, and it is an asset. One building, two identities.
存钱罐算资产吗？Does a piggy bank count as an asset?
严格说，它只是「存起来的钱」，本身不产生收入。存在会付利息的地方，利息就是资产收入；放在罐子里，它只是不会跑掉而已。书里也提醒：储蓄是用来创造更多钱的，不是用来付账单的。Strictly it is just stored money and produces nothing by itself. Put it where it earns interest and that interest is asset income. Leave it in a jar and it simply sits there. The book also warns: savings exist to create more money, not to pay bills.
那喜欢的东西都不能买了？So we can never buy things we like?
当然能。书里的顺序是：先建资产栏，让资产产生的钱去买奢侈品，而不是用自己的血汗钱先买。作者太太的奔驰，是公寓楼的租金买的，她等了四年。Of course you can. The book's order is: build the asset column first and let its income buy the luxuries, instead of buying them first with your own sweat. The author's wife got her Mercedes paid for by apartment rent — after waiting four years.

动手做Your practice

列出你拥有的五样东西，给每一样标一个箭头：↑ 往口袋里放钱、↓ 从口袋里拿钱、→ 两者都不是。标完去「资产负债分拣」页面，看看你想的和书里的标准差多少。List five things you own and give each an arrow: ↑ puts money in, ↓ takes money out, → neither. Then open the Asset Sorter page and compare your answers with the book's test.
