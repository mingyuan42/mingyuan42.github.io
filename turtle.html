海龟交易法则The Complete TurtleTrader

从零读懂趋势跟踪 · 14 节课 + 2 个附录Trend following from zero · 14 lessons + 2 appendices

1983 年，理查德·丹尼斯在报纸上登了一则小广告，想回答一个和搭档争了十年的问题：交易能力是天赋，还是可以教出来的？二十多个普通人走进教室，两周后拿着真钱上场。这门课按《The Complete TurtleTrader》的顺序，把故事、规则和风险一条条拆开讲。In 1983 Richard Dennis placed a small newspaper ad to settle a ten-year argument with his partner: is trading talent born, or can it be taught? Two dozen ordinary people took the class, then traded real money. This course follows The Complete TurtleTrader, unpacking the story, the rules and the risk one piece at a time.

07

怎么用这门课How to use this course

01

按顺序读Read in order

前五课是故事，第六到八课讲生存，第九到十三课才是规则。跳读会把规则用错。The first five carry the story, six to eight are about survival, nine to thirteen are the rules. Skipping ahead misuses them.

02

中英对照Chinese and English

每页都有中英两个版本，工具栏可切换「中文 / ENGLISH / 对照」。Every page has both languages; the toolbar switches 中文 / ENGLISH / both.

03

附录留着查Keep the appendices

附录 A 是完整规则速查，附录 B 是练习与答案，读到后面可以反复回查。Appendix A is the rule sheet, appendix B the exercises and answers. Come back to them.

本课程是读书笔记式的通识课，不构成任何投资建议。书里的规则写在几十年前的美国期货市场，今天的市场、费用与监管都不同；学的是思维方式，不是可以照搬的买卖信号。This is a reading course, not investment advice. The rules were written for the American futures market decades ago; markets, costs and regulation differ today. Learn the thinking, not a signal to copy.
