富人会「造钱」The Rich Invent Money

口袋空空的人也能看见机会吗？富爸爸说：钱不是找来的，是造出来的。Can someone with an empty pocket still spot an opportunity? Rich dad said: money is not found, it is invented.

10分钟min1个原书章节source chapters3张记忆卡cards1道测验题quiz
故事Story

这一课讲勇气。作者说，拦住大多数人的从来不是「不知道」，而是害怕犯错、害怕亏钱、害怕被人笑。所以学校里成绩最好的人，常常不是后来最有钱的人——因为学校奖励不犯错，而机会奖励敢试错。This lesson is about courage. What stops most people, the author says, is not ignorance but fear — of mistakes, of losing money, of being laughed at. That is why the best students are often not the wealthiest adults: school rewards never being wrong, while opportunity rewards being willing to be wrong.

他举了自己的例子：在凤凰城，用两万美元买下一栋标价六万的房子，首付用借来的钱，转手以六万卖出，拿到一张四万的欠条。前后大约三十小时，做成了六笔这样的交易，攒下约十九万美元的资产，每年带来约一万九千美元的收入。He gives his own example: in Phoenix he bought a house listed at $60,000 for $20,000, using borrowed money for the deposit, then sold it for $60,000 and took back a $40,000 note. About thirty hours of work across six such deals built roughly $190,000 in assets producing about $19,000 a year.

注意他反复强调的一点：这些数字发生在几十年前的美国，规则、利率、市场都不同。作者自己也说过「我写的任何一条都不推荐给你」。我们记的是思路，不是照着抄的操作。Note what he repeats: those numbers happened in America decades ago, with different rules, rates and markets. He also says nothing he wrote is a recommendation. We keep the thinking, not the trades.

他讲了一个更值得学的比喻。有人给一百个人每人一万美元。结果：八十个人什么也没做；十六个人拿去买点东西，涨了百分之五到十；只有四个人把它变成两万美元以上。同样的起点，差别在于有没有行动、有没有想法。He tells a better parable. Give one hundred people $10,000 each. Eighty do nothing. Sixteen buy something and see a five to ten percent gain. Only four turn it into $20,000 or more. Same starting line — the difference is action and ideas.

所以「造钱」不是印钞票，是三件事：看见别人没看见的机会；筹到别人筹不到的钱（不一定是借，也可能是合伙、预售、以物换物）；把聪明的人组织起来。作者说，他见过太多聪明但穷的人，因为他们只肯单打独斗。So inventing money is not printing notes. It is three things: seeing an opening others miss; assembling money others cannot (not necessarily borrowing — it can be partners, pre-sales or barter); and organising smart people. The author says he has met many clever but poor people, because they insist on working alone.

一句话The idea
机会不是等来的，是练出来的眼睛看见的。Opportunity is not waited for. It is seen by an eye that has been trained.
关键词Key words
看见机会Seeing the opportunity
同样的街角，有人看到一家关门的店，有人看到一块没人要的位置。差别是训练出来的观察力，不是运气。One person sees a shuttered shop on a corner; another sees an unclaimed location. The difference is trained observation, not luck.
筹钱Raising money
不只是向银行借。合伙、预售、以物换物、先收订金再做事，都是筹钱的方式。核心是：让别人的资源愿意跟你走。Not only borrowing from a bank. Partnerships, pre-sales, barter, deposits before delivery — all count. The core is making other people's resources willing to follow you.
组织聪明人Organising smart people
作者说自己不是最聪明的，但他会把比自己聪明的人请到一张桌子上。一个人做不完的事，一群人可以。The author says he is not the smartest person in the room, but he gathers people smarter than himself around one table. What one person cannot finish, a group can.
FOCUSFOCUS
Follow One Course Until Successful —— 认准一条路，一直走到成功为止。不是同时试十件事。Follow One Course Until Successful. Pick one road and walk it until it works — not ten experiments at once.
清单Lists
「造钱」的三块本事The three skills of inventing money
  1. 找到别人错过的机会。Find an opportunity everyone else missed.
  2. 筹到钱。Raise the money.
  3. 把聪明的人组织起来。Organise smart people.
一百个人，一万美元One hundred people, $10,000 each
  1. 80 人：什么也没做。80 people: did nothing.
  2. 16 人：买了点东西，涨 5–10%。16 people: bought something, gained 5–10%.
  3. 4 人：变成两万美元以上。4 people: turned it into $20,000 or more.
生活里In real life

班里的「闲置资源」Idle resources in class

有人美术好但不会讲题，有人数学好但画不出板报。把两个人凑到一起，就是一个「组织聪明人」的最小版本。你不需要自己什么都会。One classmate draws beautifully but cannot explain a maths problem; another explains maths but cannot draw the display board. Put them together and you have the smallest version of organising smart people. You do not need to be good at everything yourself.

预售：先收订金再做事Pre-sale: deposit first, work later

想给班级做一批手工书签，但没钱买材料。可以先画一张样品，问谁愿意订，收到订金再买材料。这就是「筹钱」，而且没有借一分钱。You want to make handmade bookmarks for the class but have no money for materials. Draw one sample, ask who wants to order, collect the deposits, then buy materials. That is raising money without borrowing a cent.

怕丢脸，所以不敢举手Too afraid to raise a hand

课堂上答错一次，其实没人记得；但你自己会记很久。作者说，正是这种「怕」让大多数人一辈子只在安全区里。试着这周主动举一次手，哪怕答案不确定。Nobody remembers the one wrong answer you gave in class — but you remember it for years. The author says that fear is what keeps most people inside the safe zone forever. Try raising your hand once this week even when you are unsure.

问一问Questions
这是不是鼓励去借钱炒房？Is this encouraging leveraged property speculation?
不是。书里的例子是作者的个人经历，不是给读者的操作指南，而且他明确说「我写的任何一条都不推荐给你」。杠杆会放大亏损，普通人照抄很容易受伤。我们学的是「看见机会、组织资源」的思路。No. Those are the author's personal experiences, not instructions for readers, and he explicitly says none of it is a recommendation. Leverage magnifies losses; copying it can hurt badly. What we borrow is the habit of seeing openings and organising resources.
「造钱」和「印假钞」有什么区别？How is inventing money different from counterfeiting?
第 2 课里两个小孩熔牙膏皮铸硬币，被亲爸爸拦下——那是造假，违法。这里的「造钱」是创造价值：做出别人愿意付钱的东西，或者把闲置的资源用起来。前者是犯罪，后者是本事。In lesson 2 the boys melted toothpaste tubes to cast coins and were stopped — that is counterfeiting, and illegal. Inventing money here means creating value: making something people will pay for, or putting idle resources to work. One is a crime, the other a skill.
为什么作者说自己不是最聪明的？Why does he say he is not the smartest?
因为他发现，聪明但穷的人往往只肯自己干。他的做法是请比自己强的人：更好的会计师、律师、经纪人。承认自己不是最强，才愿意把更强的人请到身边。Because he noticed that clever but poor people usually insist on doing everything alone. His approach was to hire people better than himself: better accountants, lawyers, brokers. Admitting you are not the best is what lets you invite the best.

动手做Your practice

这周做一次「机会观察」：连续三天，每天记下身边一件「被浪费的东西」（空着的教室、没人用的旧书、闲置的场地），然后想一个把它用起来的办法。不用真去做，先练眼睛。Run an opportunity watch this week: for three days, note one wasted thing around you each day — an empty classroom, unread old books, an idle space — then think of one way to put it to use. You need not act yet. Train the eye first.
