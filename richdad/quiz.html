Q01 · 第 1 课Q01 · Lesson 1

富爸爸禁止两个小孩说哪一句话？Which sentence did rich dad ban at home?

为什么Why「我买不起」是一个句号，说完大脑就下班了。富爸爸要求改成问句「我怎样才买得起？」，让大脑开始工作。“I can't afford it” is a full stop — the brain clocks out. Rich dad required the question “How can I afford it?” so the brain clocks in.
Q02 · 第 1 课Q02 · Lesson 1

两位爸爸最大的差别在哪里？What was the biggest difference between the two dads?

为什么Why书里说两位爸爸都努力、都正直、都爱他。差别不在学历或人品，而在他们脑子里那几句关于钱的话。Both dads worked hard, were honest and loved him. The difference was not degrees or character but the money sentences in their heads.
Q03 · 第 2 课Q03 · Lesson 2

漫画图书馆平均每周赚多少钱？How much did the comic library average per week?

为什么Why三个月里平均每周九块五，付给迈克的妹妹一美元当管理员工资。三毛钱是他们在超市三小时的工钱。It averaged $9.50 a week for three months, and they paid Mike's sister one dollar as librarian. Thirty cents was their three-hour store wage.
Q04 · 第 2 课Q04 · Lesson 2

「老鼠赛跑」指的是什么？What does the Rat Race describe?

为什么Why像仓鼠在轮子里跑：腿飞快地动，笼子一点没变。加薪常常只是让轮子转得更快，同时把开销也加大。Like a hamster in a wheel: legs spinning, cage going nowhere. A raise often just spins it faster while spending rises to match.
Q05 · 第 3 课Q05 · Lesson 3

按书里的定义，下面哪一样最可能是资产？By the book's definition, which is most likely an asset?

为什么Why判断标准只有一个：钱往哪流。租金净流进来就是资产；其余三样都在往外掏钱。One test only: which way does money flow? Net rent coming in makes it an asset; the other three all take money out.
Q06 · 第 3 课Q06 · Lesson 3

书里那句最扎心的总结是什么？What is the book's sharpest summary?

为什么Why这是第 2 课的核心句。中产的问题不是不努力，而是把负债当成了资产。This is the core line of lesson two. The middle-class problem is not laziness but mistaking liabilities for assets.
Q07 · 第 4 课Q07 · Lesson 4

雷·克洛克说他做的是什么生意？What business did Ray Kroc say he was in?

为什么Why1974 年在德州大学，他对着 MBA 学生说：卖加盟权是表面，每家店的位置才是真资产。今天麦当劳拥有的房地产比天主教会还多。At the University of Texas in 1974 he told MBA students that franchises were the surface and each store's location was the real asset. McDonald's now owns more real estate than the Catholic church.
Q08 · 第 5 课Q08 · Lesson 5

公司和员工在「顺序」上的差别是什么？How does a corporation's order differ from an employee's?

为什么Why员工是挣→交税→花剩下的；公司是挣→合法地花→对剩下的交税。这是顺序的差别，不是逃税，书里反复强调要守法。An employee earns, is taxed, then spends the rest. A corporation earns, spends legally, then is taxed on the remainder. It is a difference of order, not evasion — the book insists on obeying the law.
Q09 · 第 6 课Q09 · Lesson 6

「造钱」的三块本事不包括下面哪一项？Which is NOT one of the three skills of inventing money?

为什么Why「造钱」是创造价值，不是印钞票。第 2 课里两个小孩熔牙膏皮铸硬币被拦下——那是造假，违法。Inventing money means creating value, not printing notes. In lesson two the boys were stopped for casting coins from toothpaste tubes — that is counterfeiting, and illegal.
Q10 · 第 8 课Q10 · Lesson 8

作者说的「懒惰」最常见的伪装是什么？What is laziness's most common disguise, according to the author?

为什么Why一直加班、一直刷手机、一直说「我太忙了」，其实是在逃避。他给的解药是问自己「我想要什么？」Endless overtime, endless scrolling, endless “I'm too busy” — all avoidance. His cure is to ask “What do I want?”
Q11 · 第 9 课Q11 · Lesson 9

十步里，作者放在第一步的是什么？What does the author put as step one?

为什么Why写下你想要什么、不想要什么。没有这个理由，任何困难都能把你打回原形，其他九步也坚持不下去。Write down what you want and what you do not. Without that reason any difficulty sends you back, and the other nine steps collapse.
Q12 · 第 10 课Q12 · Lesson 10

读完这本书，最该带走的一条纪律是什么？What is the most important rule to carry away from this book?

为什么Why书里的例子带着高杠杆、特定时代和美国法律背景，作者自己反复说「我写的任何一条都不推荐给你」。学思路，别抄操作。The examples carry high leverage, a specific era and American law, and the author keeps saying nothing he wrote is a recommendation. Take the thinking, not the trades.
