The Lab · 实验室The Lab

动手玩两个实验Two hands-on experiments

第一个练「钱往哪流」的眼力，第二个练「财富天数」的算术。两个都不涉及任何真实买卖。The first trains your eye for which way money flows. The second trains the arithmetic of wealth in days. Neither involves any real trading.

01 / SORT

资产负债分拣The asset sorter

点一张卡片，再点一个桶；也可以直接拖过去Click a card then a bucket, or drag it across

书里的判断标准只有一条：资产往你口袋里放钱，负债从你口袋里拿钱。有些东西两种身份都可能——取决于你怎么用它。把下面 15 件东西分到三个桶里，分完立刻告诉你对错和理由。The book uses one test: an asset puts money in your pocket, a liability takes it out. Some things can be either — it depends how you use them. Sort the 15 items below into three buckets; you get an instant verdict and reason.

02 / CASHFLOW

现金流实验室The Cashflow Lab

教学模型 · 不是预测Teaching model · not a forecast

书里借发明家富勒的算法定义财富：不是有多少钱，而是「今天停止工作，能撑多少天」。改动下面五个数字，看看资产收入、覆盖比例和到达自由的时间怎么变。The book borrows Buckminster Fuller's definition: wealth is not how much you have, but how many days you could last if you stopped working today. Move the five numbers below and watch asset income, coverage and the time to freedom change.

这是一个教学用的假设模型，不是预测，也不是投资建议。它假设资产每月按固定年化产生收入、你每月定额投入、支出不变。真实世界有波动、税费、通胀和意外，结果会完全不同。This is a teaching model with assumed inputs — not a forecast and not investment advice. It assumes assets yield a fixed annual rate monthly, you add a fixed amount each month, and expenses stay flat. Real life has volatility, taxes, inflation and surprises, so results would differ completely.

再说一次Once more

这两个实验只教思维方式：钱往哪流、财富怎么用天数衡量。现金流实验室用的是假设数字，不是预测，也不是投资建议；真实世界有波动、税费、通胀和意外。书里那些具体的买卖例子发生在几十年前的美国，作者自己反复说「我写的任何一条都不推荐给你」。These two experiments only teach a way of thinking: which way money flows, and how to measure wealth in days. The Cashflow Lab uses assumed numbers — not a forecast, not investment advice; real life has volatility, taxes, inflation and surprises. The book's specific deals happened in America decades ago, and the author keeps saying nothing he wrote is a recommendation.
