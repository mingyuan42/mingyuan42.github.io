LESSON 07十节课The ten lessons约 9 分钟7 / 10

为学习而工作Work to Learn — Don't Work for Money

一位很有才华的记者，书卖不动。作者只给了她一个建议，她很不高兴。那个建议是什么？A talented journalist could not sell her book. The author gave her one piece of advice, and she was offended. What was it?

先讲个故事First, a story

作者在新加坡遇到一位很有才华的女记者，文章写得漂亮，书却卖不动。他建议她去上一门销售课。她生气了：我是作家，不是推销员。作者说：正因为这样，你的书才卖不动。In Singapore the author met a gifted journalist whose writing was beautiful and whose book would not sell. He suggested she take a sales course. She was offended: I am a writer, not a salesperson. He replied: that is exactly why your book does not sell.

他指着桌上那本《圣经》式的畅销书说：你看，我是「畅销书作家」，不是「好作家」。她写得比我好，但她的书卖不动，我的卖得动。差别不在文笔，在会不会卖。He pointed at a bestselling book on the table: look, I am a best-selling author, not a good author. She writes better than I do, but her book does not sell and mine does. The difference is not prose. It is knowing how to sell.

这就是第六课：为学习而工作，不要只为钱工作。作者自己从海军陆战队退伍后，特意去施乐卖复印机——不是因为他爱复印机，而是因为他害羞、怕被拒绝，而销售能治这个病。他在那里干了四年，直到不再怕敲门。That is lesson six: work to learn, not just to earn. After the Marine Corps the author deliberately took a job selling copiers at Xerox — not because he loved copiers, but because he was shy and afraid of rejection, and sales would cure that. He stayed four years, until knocking on doors no longer frightened him.

他提醒：很多人一辈子只做一件事，做到很精，然后抱怨工资低。他们缺的不是专业深度，是另外几门本事——尤其是销售、沟通、领导、谈判。他建议：找一份能学到你需要的那门本事的工作，哪怕工资低一点。He warns that many people spend a lifetime getting very good at one thing, then complain about low pay. What they lack is not depth but the other skills — especially selling, communicating, leading and negotiating. His advice: take a job that teaches the skill you need, even at lower pay.

书里还讲了肯德基创始人桑德斯上校：六十六岁破产，拿着自己的炸鸡配方一家家餐厅去推销，被拒绝了一千零九次，才有人答应。作者用这个故事说明：被拒绝不是终点，是这门课的学费。The book also tells of Colonel Sanders: broke at sixty-six, going restaurant to restaurant with his fried-chicken recipe, rejected 1,009 times before someone said yes. The author uses it to show that rejection is not the end — it is the tuition for this course.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

工资是这份工作的副产品，学到的本事才是主产品。The paycheck is a by-product of the job. The skill you learn is the main product.

三个关键词Three key words

为学习而工作Work to learn

挑工作时多问一句：这份工作能教我什么？如果答案是「什么也教不了，只是钱多」，那它可能不是最好的选择。Ask one more question when choosing work: what will this job teach me? If the answer is “nothing, it just pays well”, it may not be the best choice.

销售Selling

不是骗人买东西，是把价值讲清楚、让人愿意听。作者说这是最重要的商业技能，也是大多数专业人士最缺的。Not tricking people into buying — explaining value clearly enough that people want to listen. The author calls it the most important business skill and the one most professionals lack.

被拒绝的学费Tuition paid in rejection

桑德斯上校被拒 1,009 次。每一次拒绝都在教他怎么讲得更好。怕被拒绝的人，永远停在第一次。Colonel Sanders was rejected 1,009 times. Each rejection taught him to pitch better. Whoever fears rejection stays at number one forever.

书里的清单Lists from the book

作者认为最该学的几门本事Skills the author says matter most

  1. 销售与市场营销。Selling and marketing.
  2. 沟通：写、说、谈判。Communication: writing, speaking, negotiating.
  3. 领导与组织人。Leading and organising people.
  4. 管理现金流。Managing cash flow.
  5. 管理时间。Managing time.

作者自己的「学习路线」The author's own learning route

  1. 海军陆战队：学会领导与纪律。Marine Corps: leadership and discipline.
  2. 施乐销售：治好害羞与怕被拒绝。Xerox sales: cured shyness and fear of rejection.
  3. 房地产：学会看资产与现金流。Property: learning to read assets and cash flow.
  4. 写作与演讲：把想法卖出去。Writing and speaking: selling ideas.

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

「我是画画的，不是卖画的」“I'm an artist, not a salesperson”

这句话和那位记者一模一样。画得好是本事，让人看见你的画也是本事。学校里的板报、社团招新、义卖摊位，都是练「让人看见」的地方。Exactly the journalist's sentence. Drawing well is a skill; getting people to see your drawing is another. The class display board, club recruitment and the charity stall are all places to practise being seen.

主动申请当一次小组长Volunteer to lead once

不是为了权力，是为了学「怎么让五个人一起把事做完」。这门本事在任何工作里都用得上，而且只能在真实的小组里学。Not for power, but to learn how to get five people to finish something together. That skill transfers to every job, and it can only be learned inside a real group.

被拒绝一次，记一条笔记One rejection, one note

这周试着向别人提一次请求（借书、加入小组、请老师看你的作品）。如果被拒，别难过，写下「他为什么拒绝」和「下次我可以怎么说」。这就是桑德斯上校的 1,009 次。Make one request this week — borrow a book, join a group, ask a teacher to look at your work. If refused, do not sulk; write down why, and what you could say next time. That is your own version of the Colonel's 1,009.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

所以专业不重要？So does expertise not matter?

很重要。作者说的是「只有专业不够」。那位记者文笔很好，缺的是销售；如果你只会销售没有真东西，也走不远。两样都要，只是大多数人只练了一样。It matters a lot. His point is that expertise alone is not enough. The journalist wrote beautifully and lacked sales; someone who can only sell with nothing real to offer will not go far either. You need both — most people only train one.

工资低的工作也值得去？Is a low-paying job worth taking?

看它教什么。如果它能补上你缺的那门本事，短期低薪是学费；如果它什么也不教，只是消耗你，那就不值得。作者的标准是「学到」，不是「便宜」。Depends what it teaches. If it fills a skill you lack, the low pay is tuition. If it teaches nothing and only drains you, it is not worth it. His test is “what will I learn”, not “how cheap”.

桑德斯上校的故事是真的吗？Is the Colonel Sanders story true?

「被拒绝很多次」是流传很广的说法，具体数字在不同版本里不一样。我们用它来说明「不怕被拒绝」这件事，不必把数字当成精确史实。读任何书都要这样：抓住道理，核对细节。“Rejected many times” is a widely told story, and the exact number varies between versions. We use it to illustrate not fearing rejection, not as precise history. Read every book this way: keep the principle, check the details.

今天的练习Today's practice

写下你现在最缺的一门「非专业」本事（表达？组织？谈判？），再想一个这周就能练它的小场景。然后真的去练一次，回来记下结果。Write down the non-technical skill you lack most — speaking? organising? negotiating? Then find one small scene this week to practise it. Actually do it, and note what happened.

读完了就标记一下，课程首页的进度条会记住。Mark it when you finish — the progress bar on the course home remembers.

这是一门读书课，不是投资建议。书里的具体买卖例子发生在几十年前的美国，规则、市场和风险都和今天不同；作者自己也反复说「我做的不推荐给你」。我们只学他的思考方式，练习全部放在生活里，不涉及任何真实买卖。This is a reading course, not investment advice. The book's deals happened in America decades ago, under different rules, markets and risks — the author himself keeps saying he does not recommend them. We only borrow his way of thinking. Every exercise here happens in daily life, never in real trading.
