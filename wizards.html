怎么用这门课How to use this course

01

按顺序读Read in order

24 课分四卷，从向赢家学习、先学会亏，到风险第一、选股手艺，最后落到高手心理与自己的路。24 lessons in four volumes, from learning from winners and risk to stock picking and trading psychology.

02

先讲故事再记词Story first, terms next

每课从一位高手的真实故事讲起，再落到关键词和三道小题；读完立刻去翻卡片，趁热记住。Each lesson opens with a real trader's story, then key terms and three questions. Flip the cards right after.

03

练完再考Practise, then test

合上讲义先自己答一遍 10 个模块闯关，最后用 20 题期末大考检验，进度只存在你自己的浏览器里。Close the notes and try the module quizzes, then the final exam. Progress stays in your own browser.

本课程是对原书的整理与转述，不是原文转载。书中人物与业绩均出自施瓦格的访谈记录；交易风险极高，多数高手也经历过爆仓与巨额亏损。本课是知识讲解，不是投资建议；未成年人只需理解其中的风险意识、纪律与心理教训，不应参与真实投资。This course retells the original; it is not a reprint. The people and results come from Schwager's interviews. Trading is extremely risky and most of these masters suffered blowups. This is education, not investment advice; minors should learn about risk, discipline and psychology, not trade.
