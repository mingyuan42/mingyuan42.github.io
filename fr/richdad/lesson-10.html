走完这条路Walking the Road

十课学完了。现在把整本书收进三句话，再带上一个批判的问号。Ten lessons done. Now pack the whole book into three sentences — and carry one critical question mark.

9分钟min2个原书章节source chapters3张记忆卡cards1道测验题quiz
故事Story

书末作者说，他写这本书不是为了教你具体买什么，而是给你一套看钱的角度。他自己也承认：书里的例子发生在美国、发生在几十年前，规则和市场都变了。他反复说的一句话是：「我写的任何一条，都不推荐给你。」At the end the author says he did not write the book to tell you what to buy, but to give you a way of looking at money. He admits the examples happened in America decades ago, under different rules and markets. His repeated line: nothing I have written is a recommendation.

所以我们也必须带上批判的眼光。书里那些「两万美元买六万的房子」「六笔交易赚十九万」的做法，带着高杠杆、特定时代和美国法律背景。普通人照抄，很可能亏钱。学思维，不抄操作——这是这门课最重要的一条纪律。So we must read critically too. The book's “buy a $60,000 house for $20,000” and “six deals, $190,000” moves carry high leverage, a specific era and American law. Copying them can lose money badly. Borrow the thinking, never copy the trades — that is this course's most important rule.

书末还附了一段来自另一本书《富爸爸现金流象限》的预览。它把人赚钱的方式分成四个象限：E 雇员（为别人工作）、S 自雇者（为自己工作）、B 企业主（拥有一个系统）、I 投资者（让钱工作）。左边两个用时间换钱，右边两个让系统和钱替自己工作。The closing pages also preview another book, Rich Dad's CASHFLOW Quadrant. It sorts how people earn into four quadrants: E employee (works for someone), S self-employed (works for themselves), B business owner (owns a system), I investor (money works). The left two trade time for money; the right two let systems and money work for them.

预览里还有一个故事，是富爸爸在作者十二岁时讲的。小村没有水，村长老把送水的活儿同时包给两个人。艾德立刻买了两只镀锌铁桶，每天天不亮就跑到一英里外的湖边提水，倒进村里的水泥池。他赚到了钱，也很开心。The preview also tells a story rich dad told the author at twelve. A village had no water, so the elders gave the daily water contract to two people at once. Ed immediately bought two galvanised steel buckets and ran to the lake a mile away before dawn each day, emptying them into the village's concrete tank. He made money and was happy.

比尔却消失了几个月。他没有买桶去竞争，而是写了一份商业计划、注册了一家公司、找到四位投资人、雇了一位总经理，六个月后带着施工队回来。一年之内，一条不锈钢管道把湖和村子连了起来。通水那天他宣布：水更干净、一天二十四小时供应、价格比艾德低百分之七十五。村民欢呼着跑向管道尽头的水龙头。Bill vanished for months. Instead of buying buckets to compete, he wrote a business plan, formed a corporation, found four investors, hired a president, and returned six months later with a construction crew. Within a year a stainless-steel pipeline linked the lake to the village. At the opening he announced cleaner water, twenty-four hours a day, at seventy-five percent less than Ed. The villagers cheered and ran to the tap at the end of the pipe.

艾德只好降价百分之七十五，又买两只桶、一次提四桶，还雇了两个儿子帮忙。儿子们后来去上大学，再没回来。而比尔发现别的村子也缺水，就把这套系统卖到全世界——每桶只赚一分钱，但每天送出几十亿桶。他干不干活，钱都在流进来。Ed had to cut his price by seventy-five percent, buy two more buckets, haul four at a time and hire his two sons. The sons went to college and never came back. Bill, meanwhile, saw that other villages needed water too, and sold the system worldwide — a penny per bucket, billions of buckets a day. Whether he worked or not, the money kept flowing in.

作者说，这个故事他问了自己一辈子：「我是在修管道，还是在提桶？」这也是整本书的最后一问。你可以一辈子提桶，也可以花一段时间修管道。修管道的那几个月很枯燥、没人鼓掌，但修好之后，你睡觉的时候它还在工作。两条路，你选哪一条？The author says he has asked himself one question his whole life: am I building a pipeline, or hauling buckets? That is the book's final question too. You can haul buckets for a lifetime, or spend a while building a pipeline. Those months are dull and nobody applauds — but once it is built, it works while you sleep. Two roads. Which one do you take?

一句话The idea
提桶是今天的钱，管道是明天的钱。先想清楚你要哪一种。Buckets are today's money. Pipelines are tomorrow's. Decide which one you are building.
关键词Key words
E · 雇员E · Employee
为别人的系统工作，用时间换工资。稳定，但停下就没有收入。Works inside someone else's system, trading time for a paycheck. Stable, but stops when you stop.
S · 自雇者S · Self-employed
为自己工作，自己就是系统。自由，但一停手收入就停。Works for themselves and is the system. Free, but income stops the moment they stop.
B · 企业主B · Business owner
拥有一个系统，别人在里面工作。不在场也能运转。Owns a system that other people work inside. It runs even when they are absent.
I · 投资者I · Investor
让钱工作。资产产生的收入覆盖支出，就是书里说的「财务自由」。Makes money work. When asset income covers expenses, that is what the book calls financial freedom.

左边：用时间换钱Left: time for money右边：系统与钱替你工作Right: systems and money work for you

左边用时间换钱，右边让系统和钱替自己工作。原书提醒：四个象限都能获得财务自由，只是 B 和 I 的技能让人更快到达。The left trades time for money; the right lets systems and money work for you. The source book notes that freedom is possible in all four quadrants — the B and I skills simply get you there faster.

清单Lists
整本书，三句话The whole book in three sentences
  1. 分清资产和负债，然后买资产。Know assets from liabilities, then buy assets.
  2. 管好自己的事业，让钱为你工作。Mind your own business and make money work for you.
  3. 为学习而工作，先付给自己，别怕犯错。Work to learn, pay yourself first, and do not fear mistakes.
读这本书要带的三个问号Three question marks to carry
  1. 这些例子发生在什么时代、什么国家？今天还成立吗？What era and country were these examples from? Do they still hold?
  2. 作者自己说了「不推荐」，我为什么还要照抄？The author says “not a recommendation” — why would I copy it?
  3. 我学到的是思维，还是操作？两者能分开吗？Am I learning thinking or trades? Can I separate them?
生活里In real life

你的第一根管道Your first pipeline

不用很大。一份你整理的错题集，同学愿意借阅；一个你维护的班级图书角；一段你录的讲题视频。它们都有一个共同点：你做一次，它持续工作。It need not be big. A problem set you compiled that classmates borrow. A class library corner you maintain. A short video explaining one problem. They share one property: you build once, it keeps working.

十年后的两条路Two roads, ten years on

想象十年后的自己：一个每天提更多的桶，背越来越弯；一个花前几年修了管道，之后有时间做别的事。这不是说提桶可耻——两种都值得尊重。关键是你知道自己在选哪一条。Picture yourself in ten years: one hauling more buckets each day with a bending back; one who spent the early years building a pipeline and now has time for other things. Neither is shameful — both deserve respect. The point is knowing which road you chose.

把这门课讲给别人Teach this course to someone

第九课说「给予」。现在你可以把十课里最喜欢的一课，用三分钟讲给家人或朋友听。讲得出来，才算真的学会了。Lesson nine said “give”. Now take your favourite of the ten lessons and explain it to a family member or friend in three minutes. If you can teach it, you have really learned it.

问一问Questions
这门课到底想让我记住什么？What should I actually remember from this course?
三句话：分清资产和负债；管好自己的事业；为学习而工作。再加一条纪律：学思维，不抄操作。剩下的细节，忘了也没关系。Three sentences: know assets from liabilities; mind your own business; work to learn. Plus one rule: borrow the thinking, never copy the trades. The rest you may forget.
作者自己后来怎么样了？What happened to the author afterwards?
他继续写作、演讲、做投资教育，也经历过争议和亏损。这提醒我们：一本书的作者不是神，他的方法有时代背景，也有失败。读任何书都要带着这个问号。He kept writing, speaking and teaching investing, and also faced controversy and losses. A reminder that an author is not a god: his methods carry an era and include failures. Read every book with that question mark.
我现在该做什么？What should I do now?
去「资产负债分拣」页面玩一次，再去「现金流实验室」算一算你的财富天数。然后回到第一课，把「我怎样才买得起？」这句话用在这周的一件小事上。Play the Asset Sorter, then run your wealth-in-days through the Cashflow Lab. Then return to lesson one and use “How can I afford it?” on one small thing this week.

动手做Your practice

毕业作业：用三分钟，把这门课讲给一个人听。讲完问自己——我讲的是思维，还是操作？如果是操作，回去重读第 10 课的三个问号。Graduation task: explain this course to one person in three minutes. Then ask yourself — did I teach thinking, or trades? If trades, reread the three question marks in lesson ten.
