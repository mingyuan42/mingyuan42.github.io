周期、早期与七个问题Cycles, Early Bets, and Seven Questions

一个涨加一个跌，才算一个周期。One rise plus one fall — that is what makes a cycle.

先讲个故事First, a story

第二十二章说，韭菜脑子里只有「趋势」，没有「周期」。一个上涨加一个下跌，才组成一个周期；真正的趋势，往往要两个以上周期之后才看得出来。就像站在地球上，你觉得地是平的。Chapter 22 says leeks only have trends in their heads, never cycles. One rise plus one fall makes a cycle. A true trend often only appears after two or more cycles. It is like standing on Earth: the ground feels flat.

怎么把握周期？作者给了一个朴素办法：观察绝大多数人的情绪。牛市里人人害怕错过、开始全押时，上涨接近尾声；熊市里大家骂完、安静下来时，下跌接近底部。他还提醒：就算读完这本书，你仍有大约 75% 的概率犯错——一半可能看错周期，一半可能管不住自己。How do you handle cycles? The author gives a plain method: watch the mood of the crowd. In a bull market, when everyone fears missing out and starts going all in, the rise is nearing its end. In a bear market, when people finish cursing and go quiet, the fall is nearing its bottom. He also warns: even after reading this book you still have about a 75% chance of making the mistake — half from misreading the cycle, half from failing to control yourself.

第十七章泼了一盆冷水：早期投资不是谁都能做。失败的项目比成功的多十倍；单个项目投不了太多钱；早期意味着很长时间卖不掉。附录里，作者公开了 INBlockchain 的七个问题，用来在投任何项目之前先问自己。Chapter 17 pours cold water: early-stage investing is not for everyone. Failed projects outnumber successful ones ten to one. You cannot put much money into any single one. And early means you cannot sell for a long time. In the appendix the author publishes INBlockchain's seven questions to ask yourself before investing in anything.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

看两个周期再谈趋势；问七个问题再谈出手。Watch two cycles before you call it a trend. Ask seven questions before you act.

三个关键词Three key words

周期Cycle

涨一段、跌一段，合起来走完的一圈。A stretch up plus a stretch down: one full lap.

早期投资Early-stage investing

在一样东西还很小的时候投它，风险大、锁得久。Backing something while it is still tiny: high risk, long lock-up.

流动性Liquidity

想卖的时候能不能顺利卖掉。卖不掉，赚的只是纸上的数字。Whether you can actually sell when you want to. If you cannot, your profit is only a number on paper.

书里的清单Lists from the book

出手之前的七个问题Seven questions before you act

  1. 这个世界真的需要它吗？Is this truly needed by the world?
  2. 它彻底解决了什么原本无解的问题？What otherwise unsolvable problem does it solve completely?
  3. 真的需要去中心化吗？Is decentralization truly needed?
  4. 真的需要一本公开的账吗？Is an open ledger truly necessary?
  5. 公开账本真的能让这门生意更有效率吗？Can an open ledger truly boost the efficiency of the business model?
  6. 它在多大程度上是一家自治的公司？To what extent is it a decentralized autonomous corporation?
  7. 如果决定投，到底该用总资金的百分之几？If we decide to invest, what percentage of our fund should we use exactly?

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

四季The four seasons

只看今天升温就喊「夏天来了」，一定会穿错衣服。看过两个春夏秋冬，你才认得季节。If today gets warmer and you shout summer is here, you will dress wrong. After two full years of seasons, you recognize the pattern.

种树Planting a tree

树苗前三年几乎看不见长高，根却在往下扎。早期投资就像种树：急不得，也卖不掉。A sapling barely seems to grow for three years, but its roots go deep. Early investing is like planting a tree: you cannot rush it, and you cannot sell it.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

为什么「追涨杀跌」总会受伤？Why does buying the rise and selling the fall always hurt?

因为你看到的常常不是真趋势，只是周期里的一段波动，是幻觉。Because what you see is often not a real trend. It is one wiggle inside a cycle — an illusion.

早期项目回报那么高，为什么不能碰？Early projects return so much. Why stay away?

因为要投很多个才可能中一个，需要的资金结构普通人没有；而且钱会被锁很久。作者说：不具备条件还硬做，风险是无限大的。Because you need many bets to hit one, and ordinary people do not have that kind of capital. Your money also gets locked up for years. The author says doing it without the conditions makes risk infinitely larger.

读完这本书就不会被割了吗？After this book, am I safe from being cut?

作者自己说：还有 75% 的概率犯错。知道和做到之间，隔着「管住自己」这道最难的题。The author himself says you still have a 75% chance of erring. Between knowing and doing stands the hardest problem: controlling yourself.

今天的练习Today's practice

用「情绪温度计」练习一周：每天给身边人对某件事的热度打 1–5 分，周末看看这条线像不像一个周期的一小段。Practice with a mood thermometer for a week: each day score 1–5 how excited people around you are about one thing. On the weekend, see whether your line looks like a piece of a cycle.

读完了就标记一下，菜畦里会长出一棵韭菜。Mark it when you finish, and a leek grows in your garden.
