LESSON 05约 10 分钟about 10 min第 8 章Ch. 8第 16 章Ch. 16

缺的不是耐心，是实力You Don't Lack Patience. You Lack Strength.

为什么「拿得住」不是靠忍出来的？Why is holding on not a matter of gritting your teeth?

先讲个故事First, a story

第八章说了一句很扎心的话：韭菜不缺耐心，缺的是实力。你拿不住，不是因为脾气急，而是因为那笔钱你等着用——下个月要交学费，你怎么可能拿三年？Chapter 8 says something that stings: leeks do not lack patience, they lack strength. You cannot hold on, not because you are impatient, but because you need that money soon. If it is next month's school fee, how could you hold it for three years?

实力是什么？是你在市场之外赚钱的能力，是你的现金流。场外能挣钱，场内就不慌；场内不慌，才谈得上耐心。What is strength? It is your ability to earn money outside the market. It is your cash flow. If you can earn outside, you stay calm inside. Only when you are calm does patience even become possible.

第十六章给了一个可以动手算的式子：收益风险比 = 潜在风险 ÷ 潜在收益。想让它变小，只有两条路：把上面的风险变小，或把下面的收益变大。作者自己的选择是：努力提高场外赚钱能力，买入之后不动，穿越好几个周期。Chapter 16 gives a formula you can actually use: risk/reward ratio = potential risk ÷ potential reward. To make it smaller you have only two roads: shrink the risk on top, or grow the reward below. The author's own choice: work hard on earning outside the market, then buy and sit still through several cycles.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

耐心是实力的结果，不是性格的优点。Patience is a result of strength, not a feature of your personality.

三个关键词Three key words

现金流Cash flow

钱源源不断进来的那条小溪。溪不断，你就不慌。The little stream of money that keeps flowing in. While it flows, you do not panic.

收益风险比Risk/reward ratio

可能亏的 ÷ 可能赚的。这个数越小越好。What you could lose ÷ what you could gain. Smaller is better.

穿越周期Ride through cycles

涨也拿着，跌也拿着，走完一整轮又一轮。Hold through the rise and through the fall, all the way around one cycle and then another.

书里的清单Lists from the book

让分子变小（少亏）Shrink the top (risk)

  1. 把止损线调到合适的地方Set the stop-loss line in the right place
  2. 每一笔只占总资产的一小部分Keep each bet a small slice of everything you have
  3. 提高场外赚钱的能力Get better at earning money outside the market

让分母变大（多赚）Grow the bottom (reward)

  1. 选质量更好的标的Choose higher-quality targets
  2. 选更好的时机，比如大跌之后Choose better timing, such as after steep falls
  3. 拿得更久，穿越一到多个周期Hold longer, across one or more full cycles

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

零花钱与备用罐Pocket money and a backup jar

有备用罐的孩子，不会因为一次弄丢钱就崩溃；没有的孩子，一次小意外就是大灾难。A kid with a backup jar is not destroyed by losing money once. A kid without one turns a small accident into a disaster.

跑 3000 米Running 3,000 meters

跑不完多半不是「没意志」，是平时练得少、早饭没吃。实力不够，意志力也补不上。Failing to finish is usually not a lack of willpower. It is too little training and no breakfast. Willpower cannot patch missing strength.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

钱少就不能投资了吗？If I have little money, can I invest at all?

书里没这么说。它说的是：先把场外赚钱的能力练起来，再用「丢了也不影响生活」的钱去练手。The book does not say that. It says: build your ability to earn outside first, then practice with money whose loss would not disturb your life.

止损设多少才合适？What is a good stop-loss number?

没有一个通用百分比。标准是：这笔损失发生时，你的生活照常，你还能继续留在场上。There is no universal percentage. The test is: when that loss happens, your life goes on as usual and you can stay in the game.

为什么「拿得久」能提高收益？Why does holding longer raise the reward?

因为好东西的价值需要时间长出来。频繁进出，你只吃到波动，吃不到成长。Because good things need time to grow their value. If you jump in and out, you only catch wiggles, not growth.

今天的练习Today's practice

算一算你的「实力」：列出两项你能稳定做好、别人愿意为之付钱或给你奖励的本事。Measure your own strength: list two skills you do well and steadily, that others would pay or reward you for.

读完了就标记一下，菜畦里会长出一棵韭菜。Mark it when you finish, and a leek grows in your garden.
