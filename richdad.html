小学生双语课 · 十节课读完罗伯特·清崎A course for kids · Ten lessons through Robert Kiyosaki

罗伯特·清崎有两个爸爸：一个读到博士却一辈子为钱发愁，一个八年级没读完却成了夏威夷最有钱的人之一。这本书讲的就是两种想法的差别。我们把它拆成十节小学生也能听懂的课，每课都有中文和 English 两个版本。Robert Kiyosaki had two dads. One earned a Ph.D. and worried about money his whole life. The other never finished eighth grade and became one of the richest men in Hawaii. This book is about the difference between their two ways of thinking. We rebuilt it as ten lessons a primary-school student can follow, each in Chinese and in English.

03 / HOW

怎么用这门课How to use this course

01

先读故事Read the story first

每一课都从一个具体场景讲起：九岁的罗伯特、地下室的漫画图书馆、麦当劳创始人的那句回答。先看懂故事，道理自然就懂了。Every lesson opens with a concrete scene: nine-year-old Robert, the basement comic library, the McDonald's founder's answer. Understand the scene and the idea follows.

02

再看关键词Then the key words

每课三到五个关键词，用小学生能懂的话解释。读完立刻去翻记忆卡，趁热记住。Three to five key words per lesson, explained in plain language. Flip the flashcards right after, while it is fresh.

03

最后动手做Finish by doing

练习都在生活里：零花钱、班级图书角、教同学一道题。再去实验室分拣资产负债、算一算你的财富天数。做完标记学完，棋盘上亮起一格。Practice happens in daily life: pocket money, a class library corner, teaching one problem. Then sort assets in the lab and count your wealth in days. Mark it done and a square lights up.

读前必读Read this first

这是一门读书课，不是投资建议。书里的具体买卖例子发生在几十年前的美国，规则、市场和风险都和今天不同；作者自己也反复说「我做的不推荐给你」。我们只学他的思考方式，练习全部放在生活里，不涉及任何真实买卖。This is a reading course, not investment advice. The book's deals happened in America decades ago, under different rules, markets and risks — the author himself keeps saying he does not recommend them. We only borrow his way of thinking. Every exercise here happens in daily life, never in real trading.
