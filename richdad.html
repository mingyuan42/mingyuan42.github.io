小学生双语课 · 两条路十课A course for kids · Ten lessons on two roads

罗伯特·清崎有两个爸爸：一个读到博士却一辈子为钱发愁，一个八年级没读完却成了夏威夷最有钱的人之一。这本书讲的就是两种想法的差别。我们把它拆成十节小学生也能听懂的课，每课都有中文和 English 两个版本。Robert Kiyosaki had two dads. One earned a Ph.D. and worried about money his whole life. The other never finished eighth grade and became one of the richest men in Hawaii. This book is about the difference between their two ways of thinking. We rebuilt it as ten lessons a primary-school student can follow, each in Chinese and in English.

十节课The ten lessons

LESSON 01已学完Done约 9 分钟

两个爸爸，两种想法Two Dads, Two Ways of Thinking

一样努力、一样聪明的两个人，为什么一个留下几千万，一个留下一堆账单？Two men, equally hardworking and equally smart. Why did one leave millions behind, and the other a stack of unpaid bills?

LESSON 02已学完Done约 10 分钟

富人不为钱工作The Rich Don't Work for Money

一小时十美分，后来干脆一分钱不给——富爸爸为什么要这样「欺负」两个九岁小孩？Ten cents an hour, and later nothing at all. Why did rich dad treat two nine-year-olds this way?

→
LESSON 03已学完Done约 10 分钟

资产和负债，只有一条线Assets and Liabilities: One Line

你家的房子、爸爸的车、你的游戏机——哪些在往口袋里放钱，哪些在往外掏钱？Your family's home, the car, your games console — which ones put money in your pocket, and which take it out?

→
LESSON 04已学完Done约 9 分钟

管好自己的事业Mind Your Own Business

麦当劳的创始人对着一屋子 MBA 学生说：我不做汉堡生意。那他做的是什么生意？The founder of McDonald's told a room of MBA students: I am not in the hamburger business. So what business was he in?

→
LESSON 05已学完Done约 10 分钟

税和公司的秘密Taxes and the Power of Corporations

「向富人多收税」这条法律，最后为什么收在了投它赞成票的人头上？Why did the tax that was sold as “make the rich pay” end up landing on the people who voted for it?

→
LESSON 06已学完Done约 10 分钟

富人会「造钱」The Rich Invent Money

口袋空空的人也能看见机会吗？富爸爸说：钱不是找来的，是造出来的。Can someone with an empty pocket still spot an opportunity? Rich dad said: money is not found, it is invented.

→
LESSON 07已学完Done约 9 分钟

为学习而工作Work to Learn — Don't Work for Money

一位很有才华的记者，书卖不动。作者只给了她一个建议，她很不高兴。那个建议是什么？A talented journalist could not sell her book. The author gave her one piece of advice, and she was offended. What was it?

→
LESSON 08已学完Done约 9 分钟

五个拦路虎Five Obstacles

学会了所有道理，为什么还是动不了？书里说有五只老虎挡在路上。You have learned every principle — so why can you still not move? The book says five tigers block the road.

→
LESSON 09已学完Done约 10 分钟

十个起步动作Ten Steps to Start

道理都懂了，明天早上第一件事做什么？书里给了十步，我们把它变成小学生也能做的版本。You understand the ideas — so what is the first thing tomorrow morning? The book gives ten steps; here is the version a kid can actually do.

03

怎么用这门课How to use this course

01

先读故事Read the story first

每课从罗伯特家里的一件小事讲起，再落到那句能记住的话。Each lesson starts with a small scene at Robert's home, then lands on one line worth keeping.

02

看对照图Look at the picture

两种说法、三张现金流图、四个象限——图看懂了，概念就站住了。Two answers, three cash-flow pictures, four quadrants — the picture carries the idea.

03

练在生活里Practise in daily life

所有练习都不涉及真实买卖：管零花钱、记账、分辨资产和负债。No exercise involves real trading: pocket money, bookkeeping, telling assets from liabilities.

这是一门读书课，不是投资建议。书里的具体买卖例子发生在几十年前的美国，规则、市场和风险都和今天不同；作者自己也反复说「我做的不推荐给你」。我们只学他的思考方式，练习全部放在生活里，不涉及任何真实买卖。This is a reading course, not investment advice. The book's deals happened in America decades ago, under different rules, markets and risks — the author himself keeps saying he does not recommend them. We only borrow his way of thinking. Every exercise here happens in daily life, never in real trading.
