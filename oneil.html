12

怎么用这门课How to use this course

01

按顺序读Read in order

24 课分四卷十个模块，从 CAN SLIM 七字母讲起，到选股、买点、止损与卖出，最后落到高手的心态与自己的路。24 lessons in four volumes and ten modules, from the seven CAN SLIM letters to selection, entry, stops and exits.

02

先案例后概念Case first, concepts next

每课从一个真实交易案例或生活类比讲起，再落到核心概念与三道小题；读完立刻去翻速查卡，趁热记住。Each lesson opens with a real trade or an everyday analogy, then key concepts and three questions.

03

练完再考Practise, then test

合上讲义先自己答一遍 10 个模块自测，最后用 20 题综合测验检验，进度只存在你自己的浏览器里。Close the notes and try the module quizzes, then the final exam. Progress stays in your own browser.

本文是对欧奈尔原书的学习性转述，不是原文转载，也不构成投资建议。书中案例多为特定历史环境下的极端行情，普通投资者须独立判断、控制仓位与风险。市场有风险，投资需谨慎。This course is a study guide to O'Neil's book, not a reprint and not investment advice. The examples come from specific historical conditions; investors must judge independently and manage risk. Investing involves risk.
