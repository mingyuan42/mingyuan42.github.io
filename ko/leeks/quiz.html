小测验The quiz

十节课读完，来这里检查一遍。每题选完马上知道对不对、为什么；答错了会直接标出正确答案，回到那一课再读一遍就好。Finished the ten lessons? Check yourself here. Each answer is judged instantly, with the reason. Miss one and the correct option is marked — just reread that lesson.

Q01第 1 课 · 谁是「韭菜」？Lesson 1 · Who Is a “Leek”?

书在最后给「韭菜」下的定义是哪一个？Which one is the book's final definition of a leek?

为什么：Why: 定义跟资金大小无关，只跟脑子里的假设有关。所以钱多的人照样可能是韭菜。The definition is about the assumption in your head, not the size of your account. A rich person can still be a leek.

Q02第 2 课 · 一进场就买买买Lesson 2 · Buying the Second You Walk In

为什么「一进场就买买买」几乎是所有人的第一个错误？Why is buying the moment you arrive almost everyone's first mistake?

为什么：Why: 等你听说、心动、跑进场，价格往往已经涨了很高。By the time you hear, get excited and arrive, the price has usually already climbed high.

Q03第 2 课 · 一进场就买买买Lesson 2 · Buying the Second You Walk In

作者给「刚进场的人」的建议是什么？What does the author advise someone who just entered?

为什么：Why: 先看懂一个完整的涨跌，再动手；否则你看到的只是波动。Understand a full rise-and-fall before acting; otherwise you only see wiggles.

Q04第 3 课 · 最该练的本事：学习Lesson 3 · The Skill That Matters Most: Learning

下面哪一条是作者说的「可以马上用」的规则？Which rule does the author say you can use right away?

为什么：Why: 项目分析要慢慢练（至少三年）；借用市场的筛选可以马上用。Project analysis needs years of slow training. Borrowing the market's filter works today.

Q05第 4 课 · 这不是「你输我赢」的游戏Lesson 4 · This Is Not an “I Win, You Lose” Game

韭菜说话没礼貌的根本原因是什么？What is the root cause of leeks' bad manners?

为什么：Why: 假设决定态度：把对面当敌人，就听不进有用的信息，也就学不到东西。Assumptions set attitudes: treat the other side as an enemy and useful information stops getting in.

Q06第 5 课 · 缺的不是耐心，是实力Lesson 5 · You Don't Lack Patience. You Lack Strength.

「韭菜不缺耐心，缺的是实力」——这里的实力主要指什么？In “leeks lack strength, not patience,” what does strength mainly mean?

为什么：Why: 场外能挣钱，场内才拿得住。耐心是实力的结果。Earn outside and you can hold inside. Patience is a result of strength.

Q07第 5 课 · 缺的不是耐心，是实力Lesson 5 · You Don't Lack Patience. You Lack Strength.

收益风险比 = 潜在风险 ÷ 潜在收益。哪一组做法能让它变小？Risk/reward = potential risk ÷ potential reward. Which set makes it smaller?

为什么：Why: 减小分子或增大分母。借钱和频繁交易都会把这个比值推大。Shrink the top or grow the bottom. Borrowing and frequent trading push the ratio up.

Q08第 6 课 · 高手其实很怕风险Lesson 6 · Experts Are Actually Afraid of Risk

关于成功的交易者，哪句话是对的？Which statement about successful traders is true?

为什么：Why: 看起来的冒险，其实是控制过尺寸的风险。What looks like daring is risk that has been sized down first.

Q09第 7 课 · 止损线与交易频次Lesson 7 · The Stop-Loss Line and How Often You Trade

作者说「决定一切的根本因素」是什么？What does the author call the factor that decides everything?

为什么：Why: 频次决定手续费、犯错次数和情绪波动，所以新手第一件事是降低频次。Frequency drives fees, mistakes and mood swings, so a beginner's first job is to lower it.

Q10第 8 课 · 幻觉与「给自己找理由」Lesson 8 · Fantasies and Making Excuses for Yourself

「人怎样变坏就会怎样变傻」，共同的机制是什么？People become stupid the same way they become bad. What is the shared mechanism?

为什么：Why: 合理化会重塑大脑，让同样的错下次犯得更快、更理直气壮。Rationalizing reshapes the brain, so the same mistake comes faster and feels more justified.

Q11第 9 课 · 生活、孤独与成长Lesson 9 · Life, Loneliness, and Growing Up

抢走韭菜生活的真正原因是什么？What really steals a leek's life?

为什么：Why: 作者说区块链被冤枉了。解药是认真生活、学会自娱自乐，这样才降得下频次。The author says blockchain got blamed unfairly. The cure is living seriously and enjoying your own company, which lets you trade less.

Q12第 10 课 · 周期、早期与七个问题Lesson 10 · Cycles, Early Bets, and Seven Questions

一个完整的「周期」由什么组成？What makes one complete cycle?

为什么：Why: 真趋势往往要两个以上周期才显现；只看一段，看到的只是幻觉。A true trend usually needs two or more cycles to appear. One stretch alone is an illusion.
