幻觉与「给自己找理由」Fantasies and Making Excuses for Yourself

人怎样变坏，就会怎样变傻——用的是同一套机制。People become stupid the same way they become bad — the mechanism is identical.

先讲个故事First, a story

第十四章讲一句每个人都想过的话：「我要是在最高点卖、最低点买就好了。」作者说：这是幻觉。最高点和最低点，是极少数人冲动成交出来的，那里的成交量其实很小——而不冲动的你，注定不属于那几笔。Chapter 14 looks at a thought everyone has: if only I had sold at the very top and bought at the very bottom. The author says: that is a fantasy. The highest and lowest points are made by a tiny number of impulsive trades, and the volume there is actually small. A non-impulsive you was never going to be one of those trades.

第十五章更狠：人怎样变坏，就会怎样变傻。机制一样——做错了一件事，不承认，反而给自己编一个理由，书里叫「合理化」。理由编好了，心里舒服了，可大脑被重新塑造了一次，下次会更快犯同样的错。Chapter 15 goes further: people become stupid the same way they become bad. Same mechanism. You do something wrong. Instead of admitting it, you build a reason to excuse it — the book calls this rationalizing. Once the excuse is built you feel better, but your brain has been reshaped, and next time you will make the same mistake faster.

解药只有一句话：错了就改，绝不合理化。作者引用达利欧：痛苦 + 反思 = 进步。他还给了一个检验标准：过一段时间，如果你不觉得过去的自己傻，说明你已经傻得很彻底了。The antidote is one sentence: when you are wrong, correct it, and never rationalize. The author quotes Ray Dalio: Pain + Reflection = Progress. He also gives a test: if after some time you do not think your past self was foolish, you have already become thoroughly foolish.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

承认错误会疼一下；给错误找理由，会疼一辈子。Admitting a mistake hurts once. Excusing it hurts for a lifetime.

三个关键词Three key words

幻觉Fantasy

脑子里演得很顺、现实里做不到的那一段。The scene that plays perfectly in your head and cannot happen in real life.

合理化Rationalizing

给错误编一个好听的理由，让自己心里过关。Writing a nice-sounding reason for a mistake so your conscience lets you pass.

痛苦 + 反思 = 进步Pain + Reflection = Progress

疼过之后认真想一遍，才会真的变强；只疼不想，白疼。Think carefully after it hurts and you actually get stronger. Hurting without thinking is wasted pain.

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

抄作业Copying homework

抄完对自己说「我只是参考一下」，下次就会抄得更快。承认「我抄了」，才有可能真的学会。After copying, telling yourself I was just referencing it makes the next copy faster. Admitting I copied is the only way to actually learn.

打翻牛奶Spilling the milk

说「杯子放得不好」很轻松，说「我跑得太快」很难；但只有后一句能让你下次不再打翻。The cup was in a bad spot is easy to say. I was running too fast is hard. Only the second sentence stops the next spill.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

偶尔幻想一下也不行吗？Is it wrong to daydream sometimes?

作者说幻想很正常，他也是普通人。区别在于：你能认出那是幻想，摇摇头，继续做该做的事。The author says fantasies are normal; he is a normal person too. The difference: you can recognize it as a fantasy, shake your head, and go back to what you should be doing.

为什么「合理化」这么危险？Why is rationalizing so dangerous?

因为它把一次错误变成了一条规则。规则一旦写进脑子，以后每次遇到类似情况都会自动执行。Because it turns one mistake into a rule. Once a rule is written into your brain, it runs automatically every time something similar happens.

怎么练习「不合理化」？How do I practice not rationalizing?

写「错误日记」，只写三行：发生了什么、我做了什么、下次怎么做。不写「因为别人……」。Keep a mistake diary with three lines: what happened, what I did, what I will do next time. Never write because someone else…

今天的练习Today's practice

回想最近一次做错的事，写三行错误日记，第三行必须是一个你真的能做的动作。Recall your most recent mistake. Write the three-line diary. The third line must be an action you can actually do.

读完了就标记一下，菜畦里会长出一棵韭菜。Mark it when you finish, and a leek grows in your garden.
