十个起步动作Ten Steps to Start

道理都懂了，明天早上第一件事做什么？书里给了十步，我们把它变成小学生也能做的版本。You understand the ideas — so what is the first thing tomorrow morning? The book gives ten steps; here is the version a kid can actually do.

10分钟min2个原书章节source chapters3张记忆卡cards1道测验题quiz
故事Story

第八章叫「开始」，作者给了十个步骤。第一步是找一个比现实更大的理由：你想要什么？不想要什么？他说，没有这个理由，任何困难都能把你打回原形。Chapter eight is called “Begin”, and it lists ten steps. Step one: find a reason bigger than reality. What do you want? What do you not want? Without that reason, any difficulty sends you back to where you started.

第二步是每天做选择。他说，大多数人穷，是因为他们每天都在选择「不富有」：选择刷手机而不是读书，选择抱怨而不是行动。第三步是慎重选择朋友——不是只交有钱的朋友，而是交那些愿意谈钱、谈想法、不嘲笑你的人。Step two: choose every day. Most people stay poor because they choose “not rich” daily — scrolling instead of reading, complaining instead of acting. Step three: choose friends carefully. Not only rich friends, but people willing to talk about money and ideas without mocking you.

第四步是掌握一种模式，然后快速学下一种。第五步是先付给自己。第六步是给你的顾问付好价钱——好的会计师、律师、经纪人，他们的建议值那个钱。第七步是学会「白送」：先给出价值，回报会自己回来。Step four: master one pattern, then quickly learn the next. Step five: pay yourself first. Step six: pay your advisers well — good accountants, lawyers and brokers are worth their fees. Step seven: learn to give first; the return comes back on its own.

第八步是用资产买奢侈品。第九步是找英雄榜样——他从小把洛克菲勒和麦当劳创始人当榜样，遇到问题就问「他们会怎么做？」第十步是学会给予：教别人，你会得到更多。Step eight: let assets buy the luxuries. Step nine: choose heroes — he grew up modelling Rockefeller and the McDonald's founder, asking “what would they do?” Step ten: give. Teach others and you receive more.

第九章是「还想要更多？这里有一些可以做的事」。作者列了一串很具体的动作：停止做你正在做的事（先停下来看看有没有用）、找新想法、找已经做成的人请教、多听少说、先行动再完美、货比三家、把买资产当成买菜、想清楚再出手。Chapter nine is “Still Want More? Here Are Some Things To Do”. The list is very concrete: stop what you are doing and check whether it works; find new ideas; ask people who have already done it; listen more than you speak; act before perfect; compare prices; shop for assets like groceries; think before you strike.

一句话The idea
十步里没有一步是「等有钱了再说」。全部从今天开始。Not one of the ten steps says “wait until you have money”. All of them start today.
关键词Key words
更大的理由A reason bigger than reality
你想要什么、不想要什么，写下来。理由够大，困难才推不动你。Write down what you want and what you do not. If the reason is big enough, difficulty cannot push you back.
每天做选择Choose every day
富有不是一次决定，是每天几百次小选择的总和。今天读十页还是刷十分钟，就是一次选择。Wealth is not one decision but the sum of hundreds of small daily ones. Ten pages or ten minutes of scrolling — that is a choice.
先付给自己Pay yourself first
收入一到手，先把投资的那一份留出来，再安排其他。顺序产生压力，压力产生办法。When income arrives, set aside the investing share first, then plan the rest. Order creates pressure; pressure creates solutions.
找英雄Choose heroes
遇到问题时问：「如果是他，会怎么做？」这不是模仿，是借一双更厉害的眼睛。When stuck, ask: what would they do? This is not imitation — it is borrowing a sharper pair of eyes.
清单Lists
书里的十步（小学生版）The book's ten steps, kid edition
  1. 1. 写下你想要什么、不想要什么。1. Write down what you want and what you do not.
  2. 2. 每天做一个「变强」的小选择。2. Make one small “get stronger” choice each day.
  3. 3. 交愿意谈想法、不嘲笑你的朋友。3. Keep friends who discuss ideas and do not mock.
  4. 4. 先把一件事做熟，再学下一件。4. Master one thing, then learn the next.
  5. 5. 零花钱先留一份进「资产罐」。5. Put one share of pocket money into the asset jar first.
  6. 6. 向懂的人请教，并认真感谢。6. Ask people who know, and thank them properly.
  7. 7. 先给出价值：教同学一道题。7. Give value first: teach a classmate one problem.
  8. 8. 想要的东西，让「罐子」替你买。8. Let the jar buy the things you want.
  9. 9. 找一个榜样，问「他会怎么做」。9. Pick a hero and ask what they would do.
  10. 10. 教会别人，你会记得更牢。10. Teach someone else; you will remember it better.
第九章的具体动作Concrete moves from chapter nine
  1. 停下来，看看现在做的事有没有用。Stop and check whether what you are doing works.
  2. 找新想法：读一本从没读过的书。Find new ideas: read a book you have never read.
  3. 找已经做成的人，请他吃顿饭。Find someone who has done it and buy them lunch.
  4. 多听少说。Listen more, speak less.
  5. 先行动，再完美。Act first, perfect later.
  6. 货比三家，买资产也一样。Compare before buying — assets included.
生活里In real life

「我想要什么」清单The “what do I want” list

拿一张纸，左边写「我想要」，右边写「我不想要」。写满十条。这张纸就是你第一步的理由，贴在书桌前。Take a sheet: left column “I want”, right column “I do not want”. Fill ten lines. That sheet is your step-one reason. Tape it above your desk.

教一道题Teach one problem

第七步和第十步可以合成一件事：今天把你会的一道题讲给不会的同学听。你会发现，讲一遍比自己再做一遍记得牢得多。这就是「给予」的回报。Steps seven and ten combine into one act: explain a problem you understand to a classmate who does not. You will find that teaching it once sticks better than solving it twice. That is the return on giving.

资产罐The asset jar

找一个透明罐子，贴上「资产」两个字。每次零花钱先放十分之一进去。规则：里面的钱只能用来做「能生出更多」的事，不能买零食。三个月后看看它长到多少。Find a clear jar and label it “assets”. Put one tenth of every pocket-money payment in first. Rule: that money may only fund things that grow more — never snacks. Check the size after three months.

问一问Questions
十步里哪一步最重要？Which of the ten steps matters most?
作者把「找一个更大的理由」放第一位，因为没有它，其他九步都坚持不下去。但对我们来说，最容易立刻做的是第五步（先付给自己）和第十步（教别人）——今天就能开始。He puts “a reason bigger than reality” first, because without it the other nine collapse. For us the easiest to start today are step five (pay yourself first) and step ten (teach someone).
「停止做你正在做的事」是什么意思？What does “stop what you are doing” mean?
不是让你放弃，是让你先停下来评估：这件事还在产生效果吗？很多人一直加班、一直刷题，却从没问过「这样有用吗」。停下来不是退步，是校准。Not giving up — pausing to assess. Is this still producing results? Many people keep working overtime or drilling problems without ever asking whether it works. Stopping is not retreat; it is recalibration.
小学生也要「货比三家」？Should a kid really compare prices?
要，而且这是最容易练的。买一支笔、一本本子之前，看三家。这个习惯长大以后用在买资产上，就是书里说的「像买菜一样买资产」。习惯是从小养成的。Yes, and it is the easiest to practise. Before buying a pen or a notebook, check three places. Grown up, that habit applied to assets is exactly the book's “shop for assets like groceries”. Habits are built small.

动手做Your practice

今天做三件事：① 写下「我想要 / 我不想要」各五条；② 找一个罐子贴上「资产」；③ 把你会的一样东西教给一个人。做完在进度条上把这一课标成完成。Do three things today: write five “I want” and five “I do not want” lines; label a jar “assets”; teach one thing you know to one person. Then mark this lesson complete on the track.
读完了就标记一下，棋盘上会亮起一格。Mark it when you finish, and a square lights up.
