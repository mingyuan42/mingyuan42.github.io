完整规则速查The Complete Rules at a Glance

一页纸看完海龟系统的全部规则。建议打印出来，贴在每天看盘的地方。

一、四个基础概念#

概念 定义 作用
N（ATR） 20 日平均真实波幅 衡量市场每天走多远
真实波幅 TR max（今高−今低，|昨收−今高|，|昨收−今低|） 计算 N 的原料
单位 Unit 账户权益的 2% 每笔交易的风险预算
2N 止损 入场价反向 2N 硬止损距离

二、计算顺序（每天开盘前）#

  1. 更新每个市场的 N（20 日 TR 平均）。
  2. 算 1N 的美元价值 = N × 合约点值。
  3. 算 2N 的美元风险 = 1N 美元 × 2。
  4. 算 可承受风险 = 当前权益 × 2%（若处于减仓档位，按档位调整）。
  5. 手数 = 可承受风险 ÷ 2N 美元风险，向下取整。
  6. 若结果小于 1，放弃这笔交易。

三、入场规则#

S1（系统一，短周期）#

  • 做多：价格创 20 日新高。
  • 做空：价格创 20 日新低。
  • 出场：反向 10 日突破（做多后创 10 日新低；做空后创 10 日新高）。

S1 过滤器：

  • 如果上一次 20 日突破信号是盈利的 → 跳过本次信号。
  • “盈利”包括：你没做、但理论上会盈利的情况。
  • 上一次信号的方向不影响判断。
  • 如果上一次信号以 2N 亏损结束 → 可以做本次信号。

S2（系统二，长周期 / 失败安全阀）#

  • 做多：价格创 55 日新高。
  • 做空：价格创 55 日新低。
  • 出场：反向 20 日突破。

资金分配#

  • 通常 50% 给 S1，50% 给 S2。
  • S1 被过滤掉的大趋势，由 S2 的 55 日突破接住。

四、止损规则#

  • 硬止损：入场价反向 2N，触及即走。
  • 首日止损：建仓第一天可用 ½N，第二天起改 2N。
  • 两类出场取先到者：2N 止损 或 S1/S2 反向突破。

五、加仓（金字塔）#

  • 每朝有利方向走 1N，加 1 个单位。
  • 最多 5 个单位（含首仓）。
  • 每个新单位的止损，设在自身入场价反向 2N。
  • 所有旧单位止损，同步上移到最新单位的止损位。
  • 例：N=1，入场 100，加仓点 101、102、103、104；加到 104 后，全部止损 = 102。

六、减仓（回撤刹车）#

  • 账户每回撤 10%，单位风险降低 20%。
  • 2.00% → 1.60% → 1.28% → 1.024% → 0.819% …
  • 权益恢复、回撤收窄后，逐步调回正常。

七、组合规则#

  • 单市场单位上限：4–5 个单位。
  • 相关性：高度相关的市场（玉米/大豆、黄金/白银、道指/标普）视为同一风险源。
  • 多空净单位：净风险 = 较大边 −（较小边 ÷ 2）。
    • 4 多 3 空 → 2.5
    • 7 多 3 空 → 5.5
    • 8 多 7 空 → 4.5
  • 净单位用于控制组合总风险，不能替代相关性判断（危机时多空可能同跌）。

八、期望值与盈亏平衡#

E = (胜率 × 平均盈利) − (败率 × 平均亏损)
盈亏平衡胜率 = 平均亏损 ÷ (平均盈利 + 平均亏损)
  • 例：50% 胜率、赢 500、亏 350 → E = $75。
  • 例：40% 胜率、赢 1000、亏 350 → E = $190。
  • 盈亏平衡胜率分别为 41.2%、25.9%。
  • 胜率本身没有意义，赔率和胜率合起来才有意义。

九、每日五分钟检查清单#

  1. 各市场 N 是否已更新？
  2. 我的 2N 止损在哪里？是否触及？
  3. 是否触发 S1/S2 反向突破出场？
  4. 是否有新的突破信号？是否被过滤器跳过？
  5. 价格是否走了 1N？该加仓吗？止损是否全部上移？
  6. 当前回撤处于哪个减仓档位？
  7. 单市场和组合的净单位是否超限？

十、术语表#

术语 含义
突破（Breakout） 价格创出某周期内的新高或新低
过滤器（Filter） 在 S1 上跳过“上一次已盈利”的突破信号
失败安全阀（Fail-safe） S2 用 55 日突破补回被 S1 过滤的趋势
金字塔（Pyramiding） 在盈利仓位上加仓，每 1N 加一个单位
滑点（Skid） 实际成交价与预期价的差距
回撤（Drawdown） 权益从峰值下跌的幅度
期望值 / 优势（Expectation / Edge） 每笔交易长期平均收益
破产风险（Risk of Ruin） 在优势兑现前把本金亏光的概率
无记忆交易（Memory-less） 只依据当下状态决策，不依据历史成本或情绪
花式账户（Flair Account, S3） 可自由发挥、亏损上限 5 万美元的实验账户

十一、这本书没有承诺的事#

  • 没有承诺高胜率。 多数交易会小亏。
  • 没有承诺每年赚钱。 海龟也有连续十一个月亏损的记录。
  • 没有承诺低回撤。 组合层面可能出现 50%–80% 的回撤。
  • 没有承诺适合所有人。 它要求机械执行、情绪稳定和长期资金。
  • 没有“圣杯”。 一位海龟的原话：理查德并没有给我们圣杯。

十二、一句话总结#

用 N 量波动，用 2% 定单位，用突破入场，用 2N 止损，用 1N 加仓，用 10% 减仓，用不相关的市场分散，用纪律执行。

本课程仅供学习，不构成任何投资建议。

The entire Turtle system on one page. Print it and keep it where you look at markets.

1. Four foundational concepts#

Concept Definition Purpose
N (ATR) 20-day average true range How far the market typically travels per day
True range (TR) max(H−L, |prev close−H|, |prev close−L|) The raw input for N
Unit 2% of account equity Risk budget per trade
2N stop 2N against entry Hard stop distance

2. Order of calculation (before the open)#

  1. Update N for each market (20-day average TR).
  2. Compute 1N in dollars = N × contract point value.
  3. Compute 2N dollar risk = 1N dollars × 2.
  4. Compute allowed risk = current equity × 2% (adjusted if a de-risking tier applies).
  5. Contracts = allowed risk ÷ 2N dollar risk, rounded down.
  6. If the result is below 1, skip the trade.

3. Entry rules#

S1 (System One, short-term)#

  • Long: a new 20-day high.
  • Short: a new 20-day low.
  • Exit: an opposite 10-day breakout (new 10-day low after a long; new 10-day high after a short).

The S1 filter:

  • If the last 20-day breakout signal was a winner → skip this signal.
  • "Winner" includes cases you did not trade but that would theoretically have won.
  • The direction of the previous signal is irrelevant.
  • If the previous signal ended in a 2N loss → you may take this signal.

S2 (System Two, long-term / fail-safe)#

  • Long: a new 55-day high.
  • Short: a new 55-day low.
  • Exit: an opposite 20-day breakout.

Capital split#

  • Typically 50% to S1, 50% to S2.
  • Big trends filtered out of S1 are caught by S2's 55-day breakout.

4. Stop rules#

  • Hard stop: 2N against entry. Touch it and exit.
  • First-day stop: ½N may be used on day one; 2N from day two.
  • Two exits, first to trigger: the 2N stop or the S1/S2 opposite breakout.

5. Pyramiding#

  • Every 1N of favourable movement, add one unit.
  • Maximum 5 units including the first.
  • Each new unit's stop is 2N against its own entry.
  • All earlier stops move up to the newest unit's stop.
  • Example: N = 1, entry 100, adds at 101, 102, 103, 104; after the 104 unit, every stop is 102.

6. Cutting back (drawdown brake)#

  • For every 10% drawdown, cut unit risk by 20%.
  • 2.00% → 1.60% → 1.28% → 1.024% → 0.819% …
  • Restore gradually as equity recovers and the drawdown narrows.

7. Portfolio rules#

  • Per-market cap: 4–5 units.
  • Correlation: highly correlated markets (corn/soybeans, gold/silver, Dow/S&P) count as one risk source.
  • Net long/short units: net risk = larger side − (smaller side ÷ 2).
    • 4 long, 3 short → 2.5
    • 7 long, 3 short → 5.5
    • 8 long, 7 short → 4.5
  • Net units control total risk; they do not replace correlation judgement (in a crisis, longs and shorts can fall together).

8. Expectation and break-even#

E = (PW × AW) − (PL × AL)
Break-even PW = AL ÷ (AW + AL)
  • Example: 50% win rate, win 500, lose 350 → E = $75.
  • Example: 40% win rate, win 1,000, lose 350 → E = $190.
  • Break-even rates: 41.2% and 25.9%.
  • Accuracy alone means nothing; accuracy and payoff together mean everything.

9. Five-minute daily checklist#

  1. Is N updated for every market?
  2. Where is my 2N stop? Has it been touched?
  3. Has an S1/S2 opposite breakout triggered an exit?
  4. Is there a new breakout signal? Was it filtered?
  5. Has price moved 1N? Should I add? Have all stops moved up?
  6. Which de-risking tier am I in?
  7. Are per-market and net portfolio units within limits?

10. Glossary#

Term Meaning
Breakout Price making a new high or low over a chosen period
Filter The S1 rule that skips a breakout after a prior winner
Fail-safe S2's 55-day breakout, catching trends S1 filtered out
Pyramiding Adding a unit per 1N of favourable movement
Skid The gap between expected and actual fill prices
Drawdown The fall in equity from its peak
Expectation / Edge Average profit per trade over the long run
Risk of ruin The probability of losing all capital before the edge pays
Memory-less trading Deciding on present state, not historical cost or emotion
Flair account (S3) A free-form account with a $50,000 loss limit

11. What this book does not promise#

  • It does not promise a high win rate. Most trades lose small.
  • It does not promise a profit every year. Turtles had eleven-month losing stretches.
  • It does not promise low drawdowns. Portfolio drawdowns of 50–80% occurred.
  • It does not promise to suit everyone. It demands mechanical execution, emotional stability and long-term capital.
  • There is no Holy Grail. In one Turtle's words: Richard didn't quite give us the Grail.

12. One-sentence summary#

Measure volatility with N, size with 2%, enter on breakouts, stop at 2N, add every 1N, cut risk 20% per 10% drawdown, diversify across unrelated markets, and execute with discipline.

This course is for study only and is not investment advice.
