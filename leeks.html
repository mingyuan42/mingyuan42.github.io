02

怎么用这门课How to use this course

先读故事Read the story first

每一课都从一个具体的场景讲起，再引出书里的那句话。先看懂故事，道理自然就懂了。Every lesson opens with a concrete scene, then arrives at the book's idea. Understand the scene and the idea follows.

再看关键词Then the key words

每课三个关键词，用小学生能懂的话解释。读完立刻去翻记忆卡，趁热记住。Three key words per lesson, explained in plain language. Flip the flashcards right after, while it is fresh.

最后做练习Finish with practice

练习都在生活里：游戏、零花钱、作业和盲盒。做完标记学完，菜畦里就长出一棵韭菜。Practice happens in daily life: games, pocket money, homework, blind boxes. Mark the lesson done and a leek grows.
