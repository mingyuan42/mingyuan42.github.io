生活、孤独与成长Life, Loneliness, and Growing Up

会自己玩的人，才拿得住；脑子长大了，钱才留得住。Only people who can enjoy their own company can hold on. Only a growing mind keeps money.

先讲个故事First, a story

第十九章的标题很吓人：「韭菜没有生活」。作者说，抢走他们生活的不是市场，而是 FOMO——害怕错过。「你已经错过了这个，还要错过那个吗？」这句话对机会越少的人越管用。Chapter 19 has a scary title: leeks don't have lives. The author says the thing stealing their life is not the market. It is FOMO — fear of missing out. You already missed this; do you want to miss that too? That line works best on people who feel they have few chances.

解药是「认真生活」：把重要的事列出来，认真去做。喜欢读书就好好读完几本，喜欢弹琴就多练几首。作者说，学会自娱自乐，比交易判断重要一百倍——因为只有这样，你才降得下交易频次。The antidote is to live seriously: list what matters to you and actually do it. If you like reading, finish a few books. If you like piano, learn more songs. The author says learning to entertain yourself is a hundred times more important than trading judgment — because only then can you trade less often.

第二十章讲孤独：只有「与众不同而且正确」才值大钱。听大多数人说什么（听，不是照做），参考少数人的意见，最后自己做决定。走得越深，能商量的人越少，最后只剩你自己。第二十一章补上：生活之外还要有工作和学习，因为成长才是收益的来源。Chapter 20 is about loneliness: only being different from the crowd and right is worth a lot. Hear what most people say (hear, not obey), consult a few, and finally decide for yourself. The deeper you go, the fewer people you can discuss it with, until only you remain. Chapter 21 adds: besides life you need work and study, because growth is where returns come from.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

把生活过好了，手才不会痒；把脑子长大了，钱才留得住。Live well and your hands stop itching. Grow your mind and your money stays.

三个关键词Three key words

FOMOFOMO

害怕错过。别人一喊「快上车」，你就坐不住了。Fear of missing out. Someone shouts get on board and you cannot sit still.

自娱自乐Entertaining yourself

不用别人陪、不用花钱，也能让自己过得有滋味的本事。The skill of having a good time without company and without spending money.

与众不同且正确Different and right

跟大家想的一样，就算对了也不值钱；不一样又对了，才有大价值。Thinking like everyone else is worth little even when correct. Being different and correct is where the value is.

书里的清单Lists from the book

做决定的三步（作者朋友的版本）Three steps to a decision

  1. 听大多数人怎么说（只是听见）Hear what most people say (just hear it)
  2. 参考少数人的意见Consult the advice of a few
  3. 自己做决定，自己负责Make your own decision and own it

三样都不能 ALL INNever go ALL IN on these three

  1. 现金：永远留一部分Cash: always keep some
  2. 时间：留给工作和学习Time: save some for work and study
  3. 生活：认真过，别押上去Life: live it, do not bet it

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

一个人的兴趣A hobby of your own

有一件自己能玩很久的事（画画、拼装、跑步），你就不会一直盯着别人在干什么。If you have something you can enjoy alone for a long time — drawing, building, running — you stop staring at what everyone else is doing.

小组里的独立判断Thinking for yourself in a group

大家都说选 A，你先问「理由是什么」，再自己决定。答案可能还是 A，但那是你的 A。Everyone says A. You first ask why, then decide. The answer may still be A, but now it is your A.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

孤独不是很难受吗？Isn't loneliness painful?

作者说，一开始会。后来就像从村子搬进森林：房子不大，但整片森林都是你的院子。The author says it is at first. Later it feels like moving from a village into a forest: the house is small, but the whole forest is your yard.

为什么不能把投资当全职工作？Why can't investing be a full-time job?

因为一旦「全职」，你就没有别的地方成长了；一旦「领薪水」，你就必须每月都赢，而市场不会按月发工资。Because full time leaves you nowhere else to grow. And taking a salary means you must win every month, while the market does not pay monthly.

大多数人的话到底听不听？Should I listen to most people or not?

听，但只是「听见」，不是「照做」。大多数人的话重复来重复去，没有新信息。Hear them, but only hear — do not obey. The crowd repeats the same lines and brings no new information.

今天的练习Today's practice

列一张「我的生活清单」：写下 5 件让你开心且不需要花钱的事，本周至少做 3 件。Make a my-life list: five things that make you happy and cost no money. Do at least three this week.

读完了就标记一下，课程首页的进度条会记住。Mark it when you finish — the progress bar on the course home remembers.
