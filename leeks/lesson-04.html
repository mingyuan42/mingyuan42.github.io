这不是「你输我赢」的游戏This Is Not an “I Win, You Lose” Game

一个观念换了，人就从韭菜变成了交易者。Change one idea, and a leek turns into a trader.

先讲个故事First, a story

第五章给出全书最重要的一个观念：交易市场在长期上不是零和游戏。零和是「你赢的就是我输的」，比如猜拳赌糖。可市场里，一家公司做出好东西、一个网络变得更有用，整块蛋糕会变大。Chapter 5 gives the most important idea in the book: over the long run, trading markets are not zero-sum. Zero-sum means what you win is exactly what I lose, like betting candy on rock-paper-scissors. But in a market a company can build something good, a network can become more useful, and the whole cake gets bigger.

如果你认定是零和，就会推出一个故事：「我亏的钱，一定被某个人赚走了。」接着你会去找那个「人」。第十三章说：那个「人」其实只存在于想象里，于是大家常常把气撒到某个名人身上。If you insist the game is zero-sum, you build a story: the money I lost must have been earned by someone. Then you go looking for that someone. Chapter 13 says that person only exists in your imagination, so people often dump their anger on a famous name.

第六章说，这就是韭菜没礼貌的根本原因：把对面当成抢自己糖的人，说话当然冲。第七章说，韭菜其实也在找「价值」，只是找错了地方——他们盯的是「单价便不便宜」，不是「这东西有没有用」。Chapter 6 says this is the root of bad manners among leeks: if you believe the person across from you is stealing your candy, of course your words come out sharp. Chapter 7 says leeks do look for value — they just look in the wrong place. They stare at whether the unit price is cheap, not at whether the thing is useful.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

蛋糕会变大；真正割你的，是你脑子里的假想敌。The cake can grow. The thing really cutting you is the imaginary enemy in your head.

三个关键词Three key words

非零和Non-zero-sum

大家可以一起变好，也可以一起变差，总数不是固定的。Everyone can get better together, or worse together. The total is not fixed.

价值与价格Value and price

价值是「它有什么用」，价格是「今天标多少钱」。两者常常不一样。Value is what it is good for. Price is the number on the tag today. The two are often different.

假想敌Imaginary enemy

你认定有人专门赚你的钱，其实那个人并不存在。You are sure someone is out there taking your money. That person does not exist.

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

小组作业A group project

小组一起做出好作品，全组都加分——这不是零和。别人考砸了，你也不会自动加分。When your group makes something great, everyone gains. That is not zero-sum. Someone else failing does not automatically raise your score.

便宜的一箱笔A cheap box of pens

一箱 100 支笔只要 10 元，看着便宜，可全都写不出水——单价低不等于有价值。A box of 100 pens for 10 yuan looks cheap. But if none of them write, a low unit price does not mean value.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

短期看不是很像零和吗？In the short run, doesn't it look zero-sum?

像。所以书里强调「长期」和「本质上」。短期你买我卖，钱在换手；长期价值被创造出来，整体才可能一起变好。It does. That is why the book stresses in the long run and in essence. In the short run money changes hands. Over time value gets created, and the whole can improve together.

那我亏的钱到底去哪了？Then where did my money go?

它去了那些在你之前低价买入、在你之后卖给你的人手里——不是有人专门盯着你，是你自己在最贵的时候举了手。It went to people who bought cheap before you and sold to you after you arrived. Nobody was targeting you. You raised your hand at the most expensive moment.

礼貌和投资有什么关系？What do manners have to do with investing?

你的假设决定你的态度。把别人当敌人，你就听不进有用的信息；听不进信息，你就学不到东西。Your assumption sets your attitude. Treat others as enemies and you stop hearing useful information. Stop hearing information and you stop learning.

今天的练习Today's practice

今天找一件「大家一起变好」的事（合作、互助、开源），写下来它为什么不是零和。Find one thing today where everyone gets better together — teamwork, helping, open source. Write down why it is not zero-sum.

读完了就标记一下，菜畦里会长出一棵韭菜。Mark it when you finish, and a leek grows in your garden.
