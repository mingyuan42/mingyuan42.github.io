止损线与交易频次The Stop-Loss Line and How Often You Trade

两个旋钮，决定你能不能活到下一轮。Two knobs decide whether you survive to the next round.

先讲个故事First, a story

第十一章讲止损线：它不是随便拍一个百分比，而是要和你的实力匹配。合理的标准是——这条线被碰到时，你的生活不受影响，你还能继续参与下一次。Chapter 11 is about the stop-loss line. It is not a random percentage. It must match your strength. The test of a good line: when it is hit, your life is unaffected and you can still play the next round.

第十二章说，频次才是决定一切的根本因素。交易越频繁，手续费越多、犯错机会越多、情绪越容易被带走。作者给新手最重要的一条建议就是：降低交易频次。Chapter 12 says frequency is the factor that decides everything. The more often you trade, the more fees you pay, the more chances you have to be wrong, and the more your feelings get dragged around. The author's most important advice for beginners: reduce how often you trade.

这两件事是连在一起的：止损线管「一次亏多少」，频次管「一共亏多少次」。两个旋钮一起拧小，你才可能留在场上足够久，久到看见一个完整的周期。These two work together. The stop-loss controls how much you lose once. Frequency controls how many times you lose. Turn both knobs down and you can stay in the game long enough to see a full cycle.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

一次亏得少 + 亏的次数少 = 你能活到下一轮。Lose little each time + lose fewer times = you survive to the next round.

三个关键词Three key words

止损线Stop-loss line

事先画好的一条线：碰到就退出，不讨价还价。A line you draw in advance: touch it and you step out, with no bargaining.

交易频次Trading frequency

一段时间里你动手的次数。次数越多，磨损越大。How many times you act in a period. More actions, more wear and tear.

手续费Fees

每动一次都要交的小钱，看起来少，加起来很吓人。The small toll you pay every time you act. Tiny each time, frightening when added up.

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

每天两局Two rounds a day

给自己定「今天最多玩两局」，比「这局一定要赢回来」更能保住分数和心情。Setting at most two rounds today protects your score and your mood better than I must win it back this round.

橡皮擦规则The eraser rule

写错就擦掉重写，比在错字上继续写满整页更省时间。止损就是那块橡皮。Erasing a wrong character and rewriting beats writing a whole page on top of a mistake. A stop-loss is that eraser.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

止损后价格又涨回去怎么办？What if the price goes back up after I stop out?

这会经常发生。止损买的不是「每次都对」，而是「不会一次出局」。你付的是保险费。That happens often. A stop-loss does not buy always right. It buys never knocked out in one hit. You are paying an insurance fee.

少交易会不会赚得少？If I trade less, won't I earn less?

书里的账是这样算的：频繁交易的支出（手续费、错误、情绪）天天确定发生；而「多赚」只是可能。Here is the book's math: the costs of frequent trading — fees, mistakes, emotions — happen for sure every day. The extra profit is only a maybe.

怎么才算「频次低」？How low is low frequency?

作者没有给统一数字，他给的是方向：越低越好，低到你有时间生活、工作和学习。The author gives no single number. He gives a direction: lower is better — low enough that you have time for life, work and study.

今天的练习Today's practice

给你常做的一件「容易上头」的事定两条规则：一次最多投入多少、一天最多做几次。写在纸上贴出来。Pick one thing that easily hooks you. Write two rules: the most you will put in once, and the most times per day. Put the paper where you can see it.

读完了就标记一下，菜畦里会长出一棵韭菜。Mark it when you finish, and a leek grows in your garden.
