一进场就买买买Buying the Second You Walk In

为什么几乎所有人都犯同一个错？丢了羊之后，最该做的是什么？Why does almost everyone make the same first mistake? After the sheep is gone, what matters most?

先讲个故事First, a story

想一想你是怎么知道一个市场的：多半是听说「谁谁赚大钱了」。等你听说了、心动了、跑进场，价格往往已经涨了很高——那时候常常是牛市的尾巴。Think about how you first hear about a market. Usually someone says, “So-and-so made a lot of money.” By the time you hear it, get excited and run in, the price has already climbed high. That is often the tail end of a bull market.

所以「一进场就买买买」几乎是所有人的第一个错误：你在最热闹、最贵的时候进场，还马上把钱全换成了会跌的东西。So buying the second you arrive is almost everyone's first mistake. You walk in at the loudest, most expensive moment, and you immediately turn all your money into something that can fall.

书里给了一个听起来很笨、其实很聪明的办法：进场以后先什么都不做，只看，看很久（作者说至少一年）。第三章接着说：羊已经丢了不要紧，重要的是赶紧把羊圈的洞补上——也就是复盘、改错。The book offers a plan that sounds silly but is smart: after you arrive, do nothing at first. Just watch, for a long time — the author says at least a year. Chapter 3 adds: it is okay that the sheep is already gone. What matters is fixing the hole in the fence right away. That means reviewing what happened and correcting it.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

先看懂，再动手；被割之后，第一件事是补洞，不是骂人。Understand first, act second. After you get cut, fix the hole before you blame anyone.

三个关键词Three key words

牛市尾巴Tail of a bull market

大家都说赚钱、价格已经很高的那一段，也是最容易接棒的一段。The stretch where everyone talks about winning and prices are already high — the easiest place to be left holding the bag.

亡羊补牢Mend the pen after losing the sheep

羊丢了就把羊圈修好，剩下的羊才不会再丢。The sheep is gone, so repair the fence — that is how the rest of the flock stays safe.

复盘Review

事情结束后，回头一步步看：我为什么这么做，哪一步错了。After something ends, walk back through it step by step: why did I do that, and which step was wrong?

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

新学期第一天The first day of a new term

先看清教室规则、作业格式，再动手写第一份作业，比抢着交更省事。Learning the classroom rules and the homework format before you write beats rushing to hand something in.

球队替补席Sitting on the bench

新队员先在场边看几场，看懂配合再上场，教练反而更喜欢。A new player watches a few games from the bench first. Coaches like that player more, because they understand the team's passes.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

只看不动，不会错过机会吗？If I only watch, won't I miss my chance?

书里的回答是：机会像公交车，错过一辆还有下一辆；本金像你的腿，腿断了就哪辆车都追不上。The book's answer: chances are like buses — miss one and another comes. Your money is like your legs. If your legs are broken you cannot catch any bus.

已经被割了怎么办？What if I already got cut?

亡羊补牢。写下三件事：我为什么买？我错在哪一步？下次的规则是什么？作者引用达利欧的话：痛苦 + 反思 = 进步。Mend the pen. Write three things: why did I buy, which step was wrong, what is my rule for next time. The author quotes Ray Dalio: Pain + Reflection = Progress.

为什么要看那么久？Why watch for such a long time?

因为一个完整的涨跌需要时间才演完。只看几天，你看到的只是波动，不是规律。Because a full rise-and-fall takes time to play out. In a few days you only see wiggles, not a pattern.

今天的练习Today's practice

给自己定一条「新东西观察期」规则：任何新游戏、新爱好，先观察 7 天再花第一笔钱，并写下这 7 天看到的三件事。Give yourself a watch-first rule: for any new game or hobby, watch for 7 days before spending anything. Write down three things you noticed.

读完了就标记一下，课程首页的进度条会记住。Mark it when you finish — the progress bar on the course home remembers.
