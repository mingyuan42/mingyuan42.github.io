LESSON 03十节课The ten lessons约 9 分钟3 / 10

最该练的本事：学习The Skill That Matters Most: Learning

交易者最该掌握的能力，不是看图表，而是自己教自己。The ability a trader needs most is not reading charts. It is teaching yourself.

先讲个故事First, a story

第四章问：交易者最应该掌握的能力是什么？答案不是「预测」，而是「学习」，尤其是自己教自己的能力。市场是一所学费很贵的学校：你不学，它就一直收学费。Chapter 4 asks: what ability should a trader have most? The answer is not predicting. It is learning, especially teaching yourself. The market is a school with very expensive tuition. If you do not learn, it keeps charging you.

第十八章更实用：技能分两种。一种要慢慢练，比如「判断一个项目到底好不好」，作者说至少练三年，别急着用；另一种可以马上用。会学习的人，就是能分清这两种的人。Chapter 18 is more practical. Skills come in two kinds. Some must be trained slowly, like judging whether a project is good — the author says give it at least three years and do not rush to use it. Others can be used right away. A good learner is simply someone who can tell the two apart.

他给出一个马上能用的规则：只买成交量最大的那两三个标的。意思是：暂时放下自己的小聪明，借用整个市场的智慧——那么多人真金白银投出来的活跃度，已经帮你筛过一遍了。He gives one rule you can use right away: only buy the two or three targets with the highest trading volume. It means: put your own cleverness aside for now and borrow the wisdom of the whole market. All those people spending real money have already done a first round of sorting for you.

这一课的那句话The one line to keep

学习是本金的一部分；先学能马上用的，再慢慢练难的。Learning is part of your capital. Learn the usable things first, and train the hard things slowly.

三个关键词Three key words

自我教育Self-education

没人布置作业，你也能给自己找题目、找答案、改错。Nobody assigns homework, yet you still find your own questions, answers and corrections.

成交量Trading volume

一段时间里到底有多少人在买卖它，是「热闹程度」的尺子。How much of something actually changed hands in a period — a ruler for how busy it is.

暂时放下自己的智慧Set your cleverness aside for now

不是不动脑，而是先用市场筛好的结果，同时继续练自己的判断力。Not switching your brain off. Use the market's filter first, while you keep training your own judgment.

书里的清单Lists from the book

要慢慢练的（别急着用）Train slowly (do not rush to use)

  1. 判断一个项目好不好Judging whether a project is good
  2. 读懂一份长长的白皮书Reading a long white paper properly
  3. 形成自己的方法论Building your own method

今天就能用的Usable today

  1. 进场后先观察，不动手Watch first after you arrive, do not act
  2. 只选成交量最大的两三个Pick only the two or three busiest targets
  3. 降低交易频次Trade less often

换成生活里的事The same thing in daily life

学骑车Learning to ride a bike

先学「能马上用的」——扶稳、刹车；再练「要慢慢练的」——转弯、单手。顺序对了，摔得就少。Learn the usable parts first: balance and brakes. Train the slow parts later: turning and riding one-handed. Right order, fewer falls.

考试前的样卷A sample paper before the exam

先做一套样卷摸清题型，是马上能用的；把整科吃透，是要慢慢练的。Doing one sample paper to learn the format is usable today. Mastering the whole subject takes long training.

你可能会问Questions you might ask

为什么「项目分析」不能马上用？Why can't I use project analysis right away?

因为半懂不懂的分析最危险：你以为自己懂了，其实只是模仿。书里把它比作「小镇姑娘研究时装」，花了力气，效果反而怪。Because half-understood analysis is the most dangerous kind. You think you understand, but you are only copying. The book compares it to a small-town girl studying fashion: lots of effort, odd results.

相信市场，不就是不动脑子吗？Isn't trusting the market just turning off my brain?

不是。作者说：暂时放下「使用」自己的智慧，不等于停止「训练」自己的智慧。一边用市场的筛选，一边继续学习。No. The author says: setting aside the use of your own wisdom is not the same as stopping the training of it. Use the market's filter while you keep learning.

学习为什么也算「实力」？Why does learning count as strength?

因为你的钱会被市场拿走，但你学到的东西不会。学到的东西会帮你把下一次的钱守住。Because the market can take your money, but it cannot take what you learned. What you learn helps you keep your money next time.

今天的练习Today's practice

挑一件你正在学的事，把技能分成两栏：「今天就能用的」和「要练很久的」，每栏各写两条。Pick something you are learning. Make two columns: usable today, and needs long training. Write two items in each.

读完了就标记一下，课程首页的进度条会记住。Mark it when you finish — the progress bar on the course home remembers.
